## Authority

granted, never claimed; person, compute, model; the live record; halt on two live claims

## Fractal

same shape at every scale; inherit, write only the difference; named, approved overrides

## Leads

three intelligences; the lead letters M, P, C; every piece of work has a lead

## Why

a law without its why is incomplete; record it; never invent one

## Frame

know the aim and why; full intelligence inside the frame; report, do not act, outside it

## Lineage

the line travelled back to authority and forward to what depends; the past is not rewritten

## Missing

a gap is normal; ask why, then whether written permission exists; never invent

## Connection

lawful connections only: inherited, linked both ways, named override; agreement is not connection

## Trial

work is not right because you made it; a hostile pass from another view before promotion

## Cite

- To cite is to attach a chain of axioms. The field is `axioms:`.
- It is optional on every card and on the page stamp.
- A cite runs nothing and permits nothing.
