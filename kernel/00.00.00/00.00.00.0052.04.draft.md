## Face Jobs
source: [01 / Face Jobs]; docs/spec/kernel-design.md:94, docs/spec/kernel-design.md:96-107; why from docs/spec/kernel-design.md
why: Different readers need different representations of the same subject.

The kernel should use the same SeqAddr page architecture that it governs rather than becoming a separate, incompatible documentation format. Different readers need different representations of the same subject.

- **Face 00 — Identity:** page stamp and identity.
- **Face 01 — Law:** compact model-facing understanding and governing meaning.
- **Face 02 — Schema:** formats, templates, and precise procedures.
- **Face 03 — Operate:** compute-oriented operations, routing/menu, and authority references, where allowed by SeqAddr.
- **Face 04 — User:** readable explanation, design intent, and the preserved *why*.
- **Face 05 — Tasks:** working passes where appropriate.
- **Face 06 — Notes:** attributed observations and unresolved findings.
- **Face 08 — Map:** lawful relationships, dependencies, and navigation.

Not every page needs every face. The selected page type and its inherited contracts determine what is required. Readers should retrieve the relevant face and only the additional governing or connected information needed for the task.

The faces are different representations, **not equal sources of authority**. Conflicts should be identified and resolved by the governing agreements, not averaged into an invented compromise.

## Which Faces

## Cards
source: [01 / Cards]; docs/spec/seqAddr.md:168-170
why: 4.7

- A card is a `##` heading. Its key is the heading in title form (`Retry Policy` -> `retry-policy`), unique within a face. The same key on two faces is the same card.
- Field lines (`name: value`) follow the heading, then a blank line, then the body.
- A face's contents list is derived from its cards.

## Full Explanation
source: [01 / Full Explanation]; docs/spec/seqAddr.md:175-186
why: 4.7

Every 01 or 02 card has a 04 card with the same key, written for a person:

```
## Job
source: [01 / Job], [02 / Job]
why: Routing should reduce reading without replacing the source.
<full explanation>
```

- Hard (the compute rejects the write): the 04 card exists, and has a `why:` line and a body.
- Findings (flagged for review): the 04 body copies the 01 body; the 01 card's keywords (its brands) are missing from the 04 card.
- User pages are mostly 04. A 04 section may have a 01 card, a brief of how a model should understand it, and a 02 card only when it describes a task process. [C6]

## Counted Faces
source: [01 / Counted Faces]; docs/spec/seqAddr.md:197-209
why: 4.7

- A face is counted if and only if `FF.00` exists. `FF.00` is the board: every counter and its current tail.
- A counter without a board is invalid.
- A counted face is never seated. Plain `FF` on a counted face means `FF.00`.
- Faces 05 and 06 are always counted. Others may be counted on certain page types (for example 03 or 04 on a contents or dictionary page).
- A face without counters is seated at `DD.SS.BB.PPPP.FF`. `FF.draft` is its unseated draft. `FF.retired` is a previously seated face awaiting removal.

```
1523.05.00   board
  05.01  closed
  05.02  review
  05.03  open
  05.04  pack
```

## Counter Continuation
source: [01 / Counter Continuation]; docs/spec/seqAddr.md:257
why: 4.7

- **Counters:** once `FF.98` exists, `FF.99` points to the same face on the next free page in the same band. Counting restarts at `01`. Old counters never move.
