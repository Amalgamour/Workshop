What building and trialing 00.00.00.0051 (seqaddr) taught us. R22 (DIRECTOR-RULINGS.md:86-88): "the seated 05 for each page should explain what you learned". Every line names its source; nothing here is new law.

- Appending a ruling quote does not fix the law under it. Pass 3 (8c8f780) appended R17 and R18 to the Bands and Book Layout cards but kept the old rules, so a reader got three membership rules and an override range of 0001-0099 (docs/trial/kernel-trial-2.md findings 3 and 4). Pass 4 (fae009a) merged the old rules out.
- A mechanical word swap can break the text. The R1 merge wrote "has an door page" and left "Identity page" table headers (kernel-trial-2.md finding 10); merge-log lines were cut at the colon, losing the ruling text (finding 11). Both fixed in fae009a.
- "00 at any level means the door" wrongly covered faces. R9 (DIRECTOR-RULINGS.md:37-38) keeps sentinels to desk, shelf, book and page, and R10 (:40-41) makes face 00 the registry (kernel-trial-2.md finding 5; fixed in 0051.04 Door Rule).
- A why must come from the line it cites. TRIAL-K found about 20 whys with no line number, across 0051.04 and 0057.04 (docs/trial/kernel-trial.md:58, finding 7; examples on 0051.04 at 10 lines and on 0057.04 at 4); the builder now checks each why: against its cited line (kernel/build.mjs checkWhy). 20 of the 28 cards on 0051.04 still hold `why: 4.7`.
- The grammar on 0051.02 cannot parse the R6 axioms address `00.00.axioms.FF` (kernel-trial-2.md verdict for 0051.02; QUESTIONS Q28).
- The core applies R18 with exact titles; the ruling example `IDE-design` has capitals that the title form (A2.3) rejects (docs/BUILD-LOG.md [CORE] R18 line; QUESTIONS Q48).
- The core parser held 20,000 generated addresses and 200,000 junk strings (docs/design/DESIGN.md:57, docs/trial/core-trial.md).
- TRIAL-K3 (docs/trial/kernel-trial-3.md, commit d37e532) failed 0051.01 and 0051.04 on leftovers of the same R9, R17, R18 and R1 classes: "sets linked by number" (finding 5), "99 at any level" still covering faces (finding 6), "Every container is identified by a page 0000" (finding 9). A fix must sweep every copy of a rule, not only the line a trial names.
