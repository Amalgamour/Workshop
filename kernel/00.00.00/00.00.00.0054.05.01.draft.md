What building and trialing 00.00.00.0054 (types) taught us. R22 (DIRECTOR-RULINGS.md:86-88): "the seated 05 for each page should explain what you learned". Every line names its source; nothing here is new law.

- The page type list (system, general, code, workflow, band, pointer) names no type for an override page, a common-questions page or a door page (docs/trial/kernel-trial-2.md verdict 0054.01; QUESTIONS Q33). R19 needs a type list before per-type registry fields can be set.
- Three lists of card types drift: 0054.02 has no `question` (CONFLICTS D14); kernel/build.mjs CARD_TYPES accepts `question` and `work`; crates/seqaddr-core/src/face.rs:99-111 has `work` but not `question`.
- Stamp `type` values have no meaning given yet (kernel/MAP.md hole 4); every kernel face 00 why-draft says so.
- Whether continuation pages share the `pointer` type is copied open (0054.06.01; OPEN O35).
- 2 of the 3 cards on 0054.04 still hold `why: 4.7`.
- TRIAL-K3 (docs/trial/kernel-trial-3.md) held 0054.00 on the R19 fields (Q33), faces: form (Q39) and face00.md F2.
