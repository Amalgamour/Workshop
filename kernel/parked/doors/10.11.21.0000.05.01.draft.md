What building and trialing 10.11.21.0000 (about-me) taught us. R22 (DIRECTOR-RULINGS.md:86-88): "the seated 05 for each page should explain what you learned". Every line names its source; nothing here is new law.

- R1 "a 00seq is the door" and R10 "Page 0000.00 is the only location that exists" (DIRECTOR-RULINGS.md:4, :40): a door page holds only face 00. This pack is parked in kernel/parked/doors/ because a 05 on page 0000 would break R10 (QUESTIONS Q42).
- Before R10, door pages with no content face failed format.md:119 (docs/trial/kernel-trial.md finding 10); the law card that called a registry-only page invalid was fixed in fae009a (kernel-trial-2.md finding 1).
- Whether page 0000 has a title is a spec conflict (kernel-trial.md finding 13; CONFLICTS K17); the stamp follows decisions.md:132 and holds the container title. Title placement is held (OPEN O34, do not re-ask).
- Face 00 here is HELD-7 on the R19 registry fields (QUESTIONS Q33) and the door title (K17) (docs/trial/kernel-trial-2.md verdicts). Registry field `level: book` is the build's own.
- Page 0000 must exist for a book to be legal (D49); the builder refuses a book with no door (kernel/build.mjs validate).
