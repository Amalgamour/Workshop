# Syntax 0014.design — make the construction format carry its contract

Targets: `01.007.md` 00.md; `02.006.md` card00.md; `04.004.md` Sentinel/Find/Training. Scale: 2.

## Chain

Pack Held and face01 00.md: sentinel holds permission to exist, authority source, and control of its space → face02 promises a build format → face04 says each file can be rebuilt.

## Break

The construction instruction remains “Write three lines. What this is. The first page to open. What this is not.” The card's write line has the same three fields. Neither tells the builder how to supply the newly required authority source or control of the space; “permission: to exist. authority source. control of the space” names the requirements but supplies no field/placement contract. The signed example sentinels actually use Permission/Authority sections, which this format does not describe. A rebuild from the format can omit the expanded contract or invent its structure.

The person representation also omits consequential conditions: Find00.REG.md loses the syntax-only/explicit-need limit for another book; Training00.readme loses the desk location and explicit-need limit for a book. Those are present in01 and02. A supposedly sufficient person rebuild cannot recover those boundaries.

## Repair

Update the 00.md format and corresponding person construction instruction to identify where the required permission, authority-source, and space-control information belongs, what is referenced, and what remains unresolved. Make it consistent with the supplied sentinel structure; do not make mere self-assertion or file creation approve a container. If three lines remain a compact introduction, distinguish that introduction from the complete sentinel contract.

Carry the stated register and training placement/need conditions onto their person sections. Keep the file identity on the readable headings.

## Difference

Construction requirements and lost conditions only. Preserve address.proof as the proof, Order as the one route owner, and law0018 as the address-climb source. No copied law body, new shelf00, or required axioms on every book. The xlink-first interpretation is parked in factfind rather than invented here.
