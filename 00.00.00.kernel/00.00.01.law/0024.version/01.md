## Number
The draft number is this face's own header plus one. Do not take the number from a neighboring face. A face 01 at 009 does not make a face 02 into 010.
VVV is a three-digit axis. It has two forks. One is the version on a face. The other is a brand, a word the dictionary can identify. They are not the same number. A version does not become a brand. A brand does not seat a face.
The seat is FF. The draft is FF.VVV. The retired face is FF.version.09. The version on the retired name is the one in the old header. A lower FF.VVV is not the work.
