why: Why lead is a depth, not a second report.

Lead is how a pass becomes something the director can hold. Walk finds the holes. Lead is not that job. Named with a stem, it changes the depth. `lead.walk` is still a walk. The emit is the learning. Sync is `0017.sync`. It is not complete, and this page does not define it.

One number is one learning. The heading names the page and the face. A page alone cannot be checked. The director answers by number. After `100`, the next number is `01`. Two learnings in one number cannot be answered.

Inside the learning are four labels, and they stay inside it. Overview. Issues, only when something is wrong. Why. Ask. The shape of those labels is `13.0003` face `02`, section `00`. This page does not keep a second shape. Those four are not four numbers. They are not a page called units.

Overflow comes after the learning. It is what the pass showed that the ask did not name. If that thought would fork the work, it gets its own number. If it would not, it stays on the number that produced it. Silence after a closed pass that offered one is a miss. An empty overflow after a pass that offered none is allowed.

The ask is the last line of the number. The director's answer is the next act. A note on face `06` keeps the learning on the page the learning is about. A task on that same page's face `05` is the work that follows. It does not replace the number, and the number does not replace the note.

A return is booked the same way. The note and the task live on the page the hole belongs to. They do not live on reserved pages `0000`–`0009` unless the hole is actually on that reserved page. They do not live on `0005` because `0005` is the ticket master. A walk list dumped on `0005` is a miss. A later coldboot that opens the page will not see the return.

## Must-not
A table in place of the numbers. Two learnings under one number. Dropping a one-sided finding because the other sleeve did not see it. Making the director open a file to learn what the number meant. Booking a return onto a reserved page, or onto `0005`, when the work belongs somewhere else.
