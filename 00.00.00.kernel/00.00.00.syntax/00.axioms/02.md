A snippet is how a face is read. Any face may carry any chain. The face number does not pick the axiom.
A dot orders axioms inside one piece. A comma splits pieces. `1` is not `1.2`. `1,1.2` is two pieces.

## chain
```text
1          one piece. axiom 1
1.2        one piece. axiom 1, then axiom 2. not 2.1
2.3        one piece. fractal, then dance. not the same as 3.2
3.2        one piece. dance, then fractal. not 2.3
1.7        one piece. authority missing. a bare 7 does not say what
2.7        one piece. fractal missing. not 7.2. the writer chooses the chain
1,1.2      two pieces. first piece axiom 1. second piece the chain 1.2
1.3.9      one piece. three axioms, left to right
1,3,9      three pieces. axiom 1, then axiom 3, then axiom 9. not one chain
```
A piece is what a snippet carries. A comma does not chain. A dot does.
Left is first. `2.3` is not `3.2`. Order changes the meaning, not just the sequence.
Axiom `1` is not face `01`. A number in a piece is an axiom.

## cite
```text
axiom: 1
axiom: 1.2
runs: nothing
permits: nothing
paste: no
```
A cite is an intent key. It sharpens the work without eating the book. It does not paste the box. A cite runs nothing and permits nothing. A later axiom refines. It does not replace. Primary identifies. Secondary composes. Tertiary qualifies. The box is the boundary. The sentence under it is not the card.
