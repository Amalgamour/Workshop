## Choices

The initial header is every unfenced, unquoted line before the first unfenced, unquoted heading. When that heading is a level-1 title, the header continues until the next unfenced, unquoted heading. That next heading is the body boundary. It is classified by the existing Assignment and return-pointer rules. Every other heading is other. Header mode does not resume.

A fence, including a leading fence and a nested fence, does not supply fields and does not count as the title or the boundary. A blockquote does not either. A missing `state:` line stays null. Two eligible `state:` lines stay listed and the raw value stays null. No state word is invented. Release lines are not normalized. Duplicate `Release and claim:` and `Release:` evidence stays ambiguous.
