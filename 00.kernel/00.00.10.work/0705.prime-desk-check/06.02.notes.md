## Design decisions (before code)
- **D1. One lab, four parts.**
  - fixture builder (snapshots plus declarative ops into a new external directory);
  - a lab-local normalized observation contract with an explicit adapter boundary;
  - a checker with seven assertion statuses and a separate overall verdict;
  - a manual checklist.
- **D2. Expected results first.** `expected.json` is written and hashed before any observation is evaluated. The checker requires that hash (`--expected-sha256`), so deleting or editing assertions cannot silently pass as the same oracle.
