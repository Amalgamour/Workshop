## Choices (routine, before code)
C01. Lab `labs/prime-cycle-pass01/` with prime_cycle.py, README.md, tests/test_prime_cycle.py and demo.py. CLI:
- `read --registration ABS [--json] [--templates DIR]`;
- `record --registration ABS --decision ABS`;
- `inspect --registration ABS --work ID`;
- `packet --registration ABS --work ID --out DIR`.
C02. **Registration** `prime-cycle-registration/1`: one book, its repository, a source basis (git-blob at a commit, or live-file), the members book, a bounded list of works, the output root and bounds. Each work has:
- its page and pass, and its member;
- a goal `{goal_id, statement}`;
- a prime_review agreement;
- its review location.
The review location must sit under the page's own folder, in `evidence/`. That keeps it work-local, and it is not a face.
C03. **Review layout.** `<review_location>/<review_id>/record.json`, `REVIEW.md`, `COMMITTED.json`:
- record.json is the authoritative record;
- REVIEW.md is the readable projection, bound to the record's sha;
- COMMITTED.json is the completion marker, written last and binding both hashes.
Every file is exclusive-create. No clock is inside the record, so an identical replay is byte-identical.
C04. **Decision template.** Read pre-fills a `binding`: the work, page and pass, the source kind, commit and page sha, the member, receipt and origin, the expected prior review, and a fingerprint. Prime fills only `judgment`: outcome, disposition, pass and goal terminals, finding, reasons and refs, the next action, unresolved items, holds and answers, and the prime_review sections it needs.
C05. **Record validates.**
- A fresh observation must equal the binding; a changed page, receipt or origin refuses.
- The expected prior must equal the latest committed review.
- The terminal rules apply (below), and prime_review `report` must not refuse.
- A replay with the same id and bytes is a no-op. The same id with different bytes is a conflict. A successor needs a new id plus `supersedes`. A partial directory refuses, and recovery is a new id plus `recovers`.
C06. **Terminal rules.**
- `outcome complete` if and only if `goal.terminal completed`.
- A completed goal requires: disposition accepted, pass finished, the registration's goal_id and goal sha, at least one evidence ref, and prime_review `effective: complete-as-supplied`.
- `held` and `correction-required` require pass open and goal open.
- `director-input` requires a hold, which prime_review checks.
- A goal completion made for an old goal id or sha never applies to a changed registration goal.

C07. **The read view.** Per work, read shows:
- the source and its legacy codes;
- custody;
- the committed lineage, and any partials;
- `pass_state`: no-return, awaiting-review, reviewed, or review-stale;
- the pass terminal, and the goal terminal for the *current* goal;
- holds, with their dependent actions;
- recorded next actions, with author and source;
- unmapped legacy review text.
read never elects or dispatches anything.
