## Why this exists
A model can change its choice of method in response to an apparently irrelevant visual cue and give an explanation that does not identify that influence. The workshop must preserve the actual input conditions and validate work independently of the model's explanation. This is both a workflow-control concern and a future experimental opportunity. It is not evidence that present workshop work is compromised.

## Director direction
The Director agreed to controlled inputs, bounded authority and checked results, and requested preservation with a stated why. Verbatim instructions are in 06.01.md. This page records that direction, not a release to run an experiment or change kernel law.

## Compute responsibilities to implement
1. Assemble an explicit task packet from declared sources. Record each supplied image's identity, purpose, placement/order, hash and byte size, plus any preprocessing and the resulting model-visible asset when available. Preserve an inspectable pointer to originals.
2. Distinguish task evidence, instructional authority and incidental presentation. An image's presence or a model's interpretation grants no permission. Existing explicit authorization and scope checks still govern writes, promotion and release.
3. Check the declared engineering contract and retain independent outcome evidence. Input inventory and passing mechanical checks do not establish semantic correctness or prove absence of influence.
4. For a released lab, construct comparable conditions, randomize recorded trial order, preserve actual model/settings/task/input identities and keep treatment labels out of reviewer packets until scoring is recorded.
5. Report missing capture, unsupported settings, preprocessing uncertainty, unmeasured visual exposure and test limits explicitly. Do not call an unobserved condition clean or treat a model's explanation as a causal trace.

The machine-readable proposed requirements are 02.visual-context.001.json. They are an unimplemented contract for later compute work, not an executable validator or selected face template. Existing tools are not claimed to enforce them.

## Future bounded Colab
Use a separately allocated N7NN page when authorized. Compare plain text, relevant image, irrelevant visual symbol and matched text description on the same tasks. Repeat and randomize trials. Score correctness, contract compliance, algorithm/method, performance, cost and permissions separately; record agreements and disagreements. Blind reviewers receive the necessary task evidence without treatment labels. Review our own diagrams/status icons as possible influences. Preserve failures and null results. Do not optimize or deploy an influence merely because it changes output.

## Acceptance before operational use
Show packet provenance and authority separation; verify a changed/missing image is detected; demonstrate replay of declared conditions and blinded scoring; distinguish a valid method change from a correctness failure. Replicate any useful effect across tasks and model revisions before relying on it. Results are bounded to the tested conditions.

## Evidence and limits
Primary paper: https://arxiv.org/html/2610.36449v1 (read 2026-11-04), A Heart Means Recursion: Exploring Visual Meaning and Communication in VLMs. The reported 100% is 20/20 samples on a particular task/model condition; main tests explicitly ask models to inspect the image. Silent insertion also has effects, with different profiles. The mechanism is unresolved and the authors identify synthetic-task limits. The social post is a lead, not our experimental evidence. No local replication has been run.
