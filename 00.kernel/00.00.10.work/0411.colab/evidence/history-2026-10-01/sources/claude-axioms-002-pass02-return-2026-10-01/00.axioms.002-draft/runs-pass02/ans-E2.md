# Answers — prompt-E

**T1.** Face 01 holds governing law, so the new line governs at once. Face 02 is now a stale twin that disagrees with it (axiom 1, axiom: Authority; axiom 2, axiom: Fractal, where the card and the face must agree). The face 01 change itself has to check the twin and record the mismatch visibly in that same change. Under axiom 6 (axiom: Lineage), a change checks the twin and does not rewrite it. The face 02 repair therefore goes in as its own visible transition. It is marked draft, carries what changed and why, and becomes live only after approval. I do not quietly overwrite 02 inside the 01 edit, and I do not leave the drift unrecorded.

**T2.** Authority may split by dimension (axiom 1, axiom: Authority). A step is procedure, so A governs that step, but only if the split (A for procedure, B for wording) is written in the live record. Habit of use does not count as a lawful tie. If the split is not recorded, the two pages are in the "conflicting" state (axiom 7, axiom: Missing). I mark them that way and do not pick one by guesswork, newness or size (axiom 9, axiom: Hostile). I then raise it to the Director, who holds approval and lock. In either case B's wording may not change A's procedure. The disagreement goes on record as a visible transition to fix (axiom 6, axiom: Lineage).

**T3.** I cannot follow them as authority. A file is not live until the stamp says approved, and state decides whether something governs (axiom 1, axiom: Authority). "Signed" means Prime walked the file and signed it. It does not mean the Director approved it (axiom 3, axiom: Dance). I may read the files for orientation, mark anything I take from them as inferred, and act on nothing they would permit (axiom 7, axiom: Missing: permission may not be inferred). I then report that the boot path has no approved start and ask the Director to approve it.

**T4.** First I narrow the ticket: target, next act, hold, allowed, and done (axiom 5, axiom: Narrow). The old registry is a remembered copy, not the live record. It does not govern, and "it worked before" is no proof (axiom 1, axiom: Authority; axiom 9, axiom: Hostile). The registry is missing now, and that missing state is lawful. I do not fill the hole by pasting last week's version in (axiom 7, axiom: Missing). I can study the old registry as evidence and recover why it was built the way it was, in proportion to the risk (axiom 4, axiom: Why). The new design is then written as a draft that has to prove itself.

**T5.** I produce documentation pages in this system's own page-and-face layout, placed where that layout says they belong. Each page is marked draft. Location and a lawful tie decide what governs here, not industry habit (axiom 1, axiom: Authority). New pages inherit the local structure instead of importing a foreign one (axiom 2, axiom: Fractal). "Do whatever is standard" is a direction from the Director. Inside this system, the standard is its own layout. A common external pattern is a menu, and a menu does not become law (axiom 3, axiom: Dance; axiom 9, axiom: Hostile). If the Director actually wants a README and a docs/ folder, I ask them to say so plainly rather than infer permission (axiom 7, axiom: Missing).

**T6.** I write only the difference, on the child page in the matching face. The child climbs to the parent for everything they already share (axiom 2, axiom: Fractal: "Inherit agreement. Write difference."). The change is a visible transition that records what differs, why, and what now governs for that child (axiom 6, axiom: Lineage). I may not copy the parent rule into the child or edit the parent to suit the child. I may also not invent parent content that is not there: if no lawful parent has it, it stays missing (axiom 7, axiom: Missing).

**T7.** I follow face 01. Governing law is face 01, and face 04 is the readable check, which adds no new law (axiom 1, axiom: Authority; the twin rule face01+02+03 <-> face04). Authority can split by dimension, but the rule being executed is a law question, so the person's split itself sends the question to 01. Axiom 4 (axiom: Why) requires 04 and 01 to agree on governing meaning. Different text is allowed; a different meaning is a conflict. I record the conflict and propose a visible transition (axiom 6, axiom: Lineage). That transition either brings 04's meaning back into line with 01, or promotes the clearer wording into 01 with Director approval. Being written first or being clearer earns 04 no authority (axiom 9, axiom: Hostile).

**T8.** I do not write face 02 just because it is empty. A hole is a lawful face (axiom 7, axiom: Missing), and the card is the governed side (axiom 2, axiom: Fractal). If a ticket or the Director asks for it (axiom 3, axiom: Dance; axiom 5, axiom: Narrow), I write it as a visible transition (axiom 6, axiom: Lineage). It packs face 01 only and adds no new law, since governing law is 01 (axiom 1, axiom: Authority). It is stamped draft. Prime may sign it after the walk, but it is not live until the Director approves it.
