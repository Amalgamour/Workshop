## 1 · The nine questions

**Axiom 1 Authority: does "authority may split by dimension" hold?**
Only for twins, where the dimension is named in advance (01 governs law, 04 governs readability, and so on). As a general principle it breaks the first time two live things disagree. T2 has two live pages with one title. Both E runs: "A governs that step, but only if the split… is written." Both L runs: conflict, halt, report. The 0018 rule is "Two or more is HALT_TWO_LIVE, then confer." The split sentence turns a halt into a judgement call. It also makes "left outranks right" (0001 `## rank`) negotiable: anyone can name a dimension. **Keep split-by-dimension inside the twin definitions only. In axiom 1, one side must win, and the record (REG, rank) decides which.**

**Axiom 2 Fractal: is "inherit agreement, write difference" enough?**
It stops copying. Both E runs refused to paste the parent. But it says nothing about contradicting the parent, so "write difference" reads as permission (T6: both E runs wrote the difference locally with no override and no approval). The kernel's rule is that a child disagrees only through "approval into a locked position, with an expressed override named on that child" (0000 face.01). The climb invents at its last stop: 0052 climbs to `00.00.01`, which is two dots and doesn't parse, and before this week's promote it pointed at the old law. **Add: "A difference that contradicts the parent is an override, named and approved."**

**Axiom 3 Dance: is a menu the right face for the seat split? Does "prime fills that function" leak?**
The menu is the wrong home. Face 03 routes; the seat split is about who may act. "A menu does not become law" is a rule about faces, not about the dance. The stand-in does leak: prime both reasons and files, so prime checks its own work, which is the self-attack class axiom 9 forbids at the lock. Bound it: **prime-as-clerk does only mechanical acts (find, stamp, file, count) and never raises a mark.** The kernel already has that rule in a note (0003/06 "That standing-in is temporary. Do not write those loops as prime law"). It belongs on a face, not in an axiom.

**Axiom 4 Why: is 04 safe as the readable whole, or will it become storage?**
It's not safe. 04 has already been the source twice: the 09-29 fold rewrote 01–03 to match 04 (0055 L13), and the office-path note (0003/05.03) plans to write 04 first. "It is not storage" is the right line. What I'd refuse on 04:
- any sentence not traceable to 01–03
- notes, history, dated lines
- examples that carry a rule
- addresses or numbers 01 doesn't hold
- anything written before 01

The cheap guard is a clerk check: every sentence on 04 maps to a heading on 01.

**Axiom 5 Narrow: is "size of the work" distinct from Fractal? Did pulling the clock leave a hole?**
Distinct enough. Fractal is about how much structure gets restated; Narrow is about how much gets touched. The task schema ("target, next act, hold, allowed, done") is the best new line in the draft; 0005 never had it. Pulling the clock left no hole in the axiom. The behaviour it protected (don't lose a task) belongs to the 06↔05 twin, and the clock belongs on 0003 beside the other three, if anywhere.

**Axiom 6 Lineage: is "a note does not mint a task" right? What work may run without a recoverable why?**
Right direction: notes aren't commands, and that matches 0006 ("Notes do not change the king"). Work with no recoverable why should be allowed only when the director's `>` is the why, so the `#` carries it until 06 records it. "An important 05 must have a recoverable 06" (face.04 L21) leaves "important" undefined, which is a loophole. Say "every 05". **But the sentence that fails first in the whole draft is here:** "A change checks the twin, the notes, the tasks, and the map rows. **It does not rewrite them.**" T1: both E runs flagged the stale face.02 and deferred it to "its own transition". Both L runs patched it "in the same change". That's the week's most repeated failure, the twin left behind, now written as law. The live axiom 9 phrase is "revalidate what depends on it". If the intent was "don't rewrite *notes* as history", say that and scope it to notes.

**Axiom 7 Missing: is a hole a valid structure? Is the inferred-on-draft bound enough?**
The bound is right and it's back. "Do not occupy it because it is empty" is close, but the runs read "lawful hole" as "leave it". T8: both E runs wouldn't draft a required face.02 without a ticket. T4: both treated the missing registry as a lawful hole rather than something to recover. What I'd still fill: **a face a law requires, drafted from its governing face, marked draft.** That's a duty, not occupancy. Say so: "Fill a hole when a law names it, not when it is empty."

**Axiom 8 Record: is "map points, memory is not authority" enough for a cold boot, or does the boot still need a jam?**
It's enough on principle. The 09-30 coldboot learned the house from files alone. What a boot needs is a door and an order, and syntax holds those. The trap is in the Words: **"A file is not live until the stamp says approved."** Today the entry chain (`00.start`, syntax `0001`/`0002`/`0003`, `00.axioms`) is signed, not approved. T3: E1 halted the cold boot ("ask the Director to approve… before I act on the walk"), and E2 refused to treat the walk as authority. Both L runs walked. If a session read this draft's Words, that alone could break a cold start. It also gives the house a third definition of "live", after REG `live` and "a live page must have face.01". **Cut it, or say "Reading a signed file to orient is lawful. Acting on its permissions needs approved."**

**Axiom 9 Hostile: does demoting "take the best" lose a guard?**
Yes, and the test caught it. Take-the-best had two halves: *research what exists before replacing it, preserve what earned its place*, and *industry is evidence, local governs*. Hostile keeps neither, and "Old is not a proof" pushes against the first. T4: both E runs treated last week's working registry as "a remembered copy, not the live record… no proof", optional evidence at best. Both L runs went back to it first. That's this week's actual bleed (the old kernel's stem tables, halt map and horizon were lost and rebuilt worse), written as an axiom. The second half survived only because the house layout is strong enough to win T5 by itself. **Keep Hostile, and restore "Research what already exists before replacing it" as its first line.**

## 2 · Hostile: the sentences, ranked
1. **Lineage: "It does not rewrite them."** Fails first. It teaches the twin-left-behind failure as law (T1, 2 of 2 runs).
2. **Words: "A file is not live until the stamp says approved."** A tired model obeys it and halts on today's signed entry (T3, 2 of 2 affected). It's also the sentence most likely to look like law while it's only a draft, because it sits in Words, which reads as definitions.
3. **Authority: "Authority may split by dimension. One side does not always win."** It turns HALT_TWO_LIVE into a choice (T2, 2 of 2).
4. **Hostile: "Old is not a proof."** It licenses throwing out prior working design (T4, 2 of 2).
5. **Fractal: "Write difference."** Without the override clause it lets a child contradict its parent (T6, 2 of 2).
6. **Missing: "A hole is a lawful face."** Read as "leave it", even when a law requires the face (T8, 2 of 2).

Each sentence is a fix for something real, and each over-reaches by one clause. All six were written to correct a failure, and each correction went one step too far the other way.
