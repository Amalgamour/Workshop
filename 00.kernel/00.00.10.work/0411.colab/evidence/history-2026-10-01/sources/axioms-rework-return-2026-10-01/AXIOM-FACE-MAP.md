# AXIOM ↔ FACE MAP

| # | Axiom | Primary face | Short operational test |
|---|---|---:|---|
| 1 | Authority | 01 | What lawfully governs here? |
| 2 | Fractal | 02 | Can I inherit this instead of duplicating it? |
| 3 | Dance | 03 | Is each actor/representation doing only its job? |
| 4 | Why | 04 | Can the reason/design meaning be recovered? |
| 5 | Narrow | 05 | Is the work bounded before intelligence is spent? |
| 6 | Lineage | 06 | Can I recover what changed and why? |
| 7 | Missing | 07 | Am I preserving absence instead of inventing? |
| 8 | Record | 08 | Can I find the maintained governing source? |
| 9 | Attack | 09 / transition | Has this authority transition survived independent challenge? |

## Compact interaction

```text
READ
Record -> Authority -> Fractal -> Missing -> Why

WORK
Dance -> Narrow

CHANGE
Lineage -> Attack

REPEAT
```

## Core pairings

```text
Fractal + Missing
climb for agreement + never invent absent truth

Authority + Record
what governs + where to recover it

Why + Lineage
why it exists + why/how it changed

Dance + Narrow
who should act + smallest lawful work

Attack
protects the boundary when authority changes
```
