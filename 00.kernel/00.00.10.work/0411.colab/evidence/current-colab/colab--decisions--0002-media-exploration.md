## D3 /#05 — campaign art is deliberately sparse
reply: "I think a campaign having a handful of imagines is enough. So a big event like the final conflict might have one of a battle, one of the last boss, a victory image. But we don't want a ton of imagines. Nor should the be a large imagine either."
Decision:a SMALL CAMPAIGN-WIDE art budget, with small images. Picture requests are presentation/out-of-character and don't consume story turns, but they do not bypass the campaign budget. This supersedes Prime's earlier suggestion of no fixed campaign quota. Existing artwork is recalled first. One major story beat can justify several distinct planned images (battle/boss/victory), each counted. Numeric count/dimensions/bytes were NOT specified by Director; expose explicit policy and label fixture numbers as examples, not an approved universal limit. No unsolicited generation flood or one-picture-per-scene assumption.
