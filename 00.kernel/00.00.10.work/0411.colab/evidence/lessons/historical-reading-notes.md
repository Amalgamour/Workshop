## Central lesson
A Colab is a structured investigation of a design. It can include controlled/blind tests, then use intelligence to choose sharper experiments and explain why a design works/fails. It need not be unstructured discussion, and it is not merely a model qualification exam. My earlier contrast between Colab and controlled tests was too simple. Purpose is diagnosis and design improvement; workflow preserves scope, sources, evidence and transitions.

## Particularly useful mechanisms
COLAB.md gives project significance, artifact reading order and one concrete question per axiom. The hostile pass asks for the first failing sentence and what a tired model might mistake for law. Overflow protects relevant discoveries outside the question list. Unprompted asks what to add/cut/refuse without rewriting the source. Returns separate architectural assessment, wording, promotion and next narrow pass.
Claude's broad first comparison reported near ties (live30/30, draft29/30). The next targeted scenario set reported live16/16 versus draft9/10, traced to repeated failures on specific sentences. That is a lesson in choosing discriminating cases rather than accepting easy high scores. Retained prompts/rubrics/raw answers support auditing those claims. Small sample sizes, bundled wording changes, scenario scope and grader criteria limit generalization; the outputs do not prove a model-wide improvement or perfectly isolated sentence causality.

## Concrete failures relevant now
'It does not rewrite them' caused stale dependent02 to be deferred. 'Not live until approved' stalled reading signed boot sources. 'A lawful hole' encouraged refusing to draft a required face. 'Write difference' lacked the distinction between local specialization and a parent contradiction requiring approval. Each guard corrected one failure and overreached into a nearby useful behavior. A hostile Colab should test both what the guard prevents and what legitimate work it may block.

## Prime's role is visible in annotated04
The notes retain differing model views, named provenance, unresolved forks and stronger/weaker assessments. They are not votes. Prime/Director compare critiques and retain the reason for a choice; no whole-return automatic promotion. An insightful recommendation is still a proposal, including flattering architectural claims in earlier returns. Later tests may overturn them.

## Current application
Use current Prime tools in actual work first. When desk-state view returns, a Colab can reconstruct a morning situation, change one fixture, introduce a pending return or stale judgment, and explain where it misleads or wastes effort. Preserve Hostile/Overflow/Unprompted and constructive alternatives. A useful return may say the first test did not distinguish alternatives and propose a better one. Do not restart old axioms, adopt packed digit grammar or reinstate old07 semantics from historical evidence. Current local0007/07 decisions remain separate.
