## Prime working interpretation — proposals for discussion
Colab is another intelligence brought into a bounded design/problem: understand purpose and artifacts, explain its reading, exercise examples, challenge assumptions, seek counterexamples, expose missing context and return evidence/reasoning. It can ask questions and explore relevant implications; it does not need to follow a hidden answer key. A hostile pass tries to break the design, not merely criticise wording. A formal benchmark is a separate declared mode only when its controls answer the actual research question.
Prime should choose a live uncertainty; supply self-contained purpose/context/current artifacts/known limits/environment; ask for independent reconstruction and failure cases; distinguish observed operations from proposals; reconcile findings with Director intent; assign accepted bounded implementation through workflow; and preserve raw return, exact Director replies, Prime disposition, resulting changes and reusable lessons.
Workflow provides assignment, source, evidence, custody, review and publication mechanics. It carries a Colab rather than defining the full intellectual activity of one. A model may serve Colab or Builder on different scoped tasks; role and permissions must be explicit.
