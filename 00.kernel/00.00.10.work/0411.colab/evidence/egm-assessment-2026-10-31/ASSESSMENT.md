## Verified upstream behavior
Deep-context author, conversation-isolated reviewers with referenced sources,
and a human resolving design trade-offs are the central roles. Five review
stages cover simplicity, comprehension, criticism, simplicity again and
implementation readiness. Presets can run selected portions. Comprehension
keeps its paraphrase, not only defect findings. Review-loop/synthesis records
failed reviewers as not run rather than zero findings. Reports and artifacts
are written to files. TESTING.md documents mechanical tests and native workflow/
isolation checks with incomplete surface-specific evidence explicitly retained.
These are documentation observations, not tests Prime executed on EGM.

## Interpretation and fit
This overlaps our use of a context-holder, independent readers, recorded work
and Director judgment. Colab is broader: contextual design/exploration, builds,
simulation and cold-reader tests are distinct methods. Prior familiarity and
actual supplied context must be recorded. Fresh conversation is not absence of
general software knowledge; reviewers need a bounded adequate source packet.
Workshop emphasizes addresses, immutable custody and recorded authority, but
recent dependency and false-ready failures preclude asserting proven superiority.
EGM is not merely untested prompts; its own verification work is worth studying.

## Proposed adoption
1. Selectable review modes/stages, not five compulsory reviews for every fix.
2. Comprehension: explain the proposed behavior, current system, task, authority,
   finish boundary and return route before critique. Keep the complete answer.
3. After substantial revisions, assess complexity introduced by the fixes.
4. Synthesize with original finding identities, reviewer/source links,
   disagreement and disposition preserved. Failed/missing review stays incomplete.
Models provide meaning; compute checks records, identity, declared coverage and
linkage. No mechanical completeness label establishes semantic adequacy.

## Do not adopt unchanged
One egm-review pause/resume path advances residual findings as accepted trade-offs
after its re-audit cap. Exhausted review effort cannot create Director acceptance.
The separate review-loop path instead asks the human at cap/churn; preserve that
distinction when discussing upstream behavior. Do not copy blanket critique-first
or artifact-only chat interaction into our Director discussion loop. Do not replace
useful exploration with compulsory adversarial performance or manufactured defects.

## Drift boundary
The upstream drift skill is a model-led document/code comparison, not a built-in
weekly scheduler. Compare the applicable accepted contract and selected implementation,
not every future proposal against current code. Compute can identify changed/missing
files; Prime/Observer judge lost meaning. Defer a separate drift module until the
dependency/provenance work identifies the correct subjects. No automation is created.
