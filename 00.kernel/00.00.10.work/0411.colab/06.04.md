# Cold review before kernel promotion
date: 2026-11-02
source: Director verbatim agreement at 0412.prime-review/06.07
state: Director development agreement; not a dispatched Colab or kernel release

A new kernel piece cannot be promoted until a genuinely cold review of the selected candidate meaning has been recorded and its findings disposed of. Prepare it before final approval when practical, then reuse the bound evidence at promotion while the meaning and applicable sources stay unchanged. This applies the existing ready-for-testing-before-release trigger; it does not add cold reviews to every routine return.

Prime prepares one frozen brief with exact candidate and seated source law, relevant Director decisions, intended task and minimal required dependencies. Withhold author returns, chat and Prime verdict until first findings are preserved. Do not omit real authority boundaries. Record model/family, context exposure, prior involvement, input identities and limitations. A persistent familiar session/bot is not made cold by an instruction. The Director proposed the other two frontier models: split one reader toward semantic preservation and the other toward contradictions/failure cases, with separate initial returns and later synthesis. The handoff is a design for explicit dispatch, not permission to invoke models now. If a second view is omitted for budget, record that limitation; the cold promotion gate itself is not waived.

Author-written cases, reviewer-written hostile probes and reproduced past failures remain separate evidence classes. Prime can add local independent probes without launching another model for ordinary critical behavior reviews. Independent coverage is substantive, not a magic case count. Prima/bot use is deferred for reconsideration after the Thursday reset; do not schedule or resume it automatically.
