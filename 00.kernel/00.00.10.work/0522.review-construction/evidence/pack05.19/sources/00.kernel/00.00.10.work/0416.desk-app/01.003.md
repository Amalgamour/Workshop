# Workshop application - updated understanding
version: 003

who: Codex Prime recording Director discussion

## Preservation and present state
Preserve work, decisions, source evidence and reasons. Current selected state must
be explicit, with exact history retrievable. Newest file is not automatically current.
Addressed books/pages hold the record; compute constructs their required structure.
Preserve Director wording and why, not only the derived instruction or summary.

## Inspectability is a deliverable
The Director must be able to open any piece and understand how and why it works.
For an operation/result, expose its originating request/task, exact source versions,
applicable kernel/contract rules, actual compute operations, model-supplied judgment,
changes, checks, outcome, authority/release evidence, unresolved issues and next route.
Provide a readable view with links through to durable records and raw evidence.
Do not build the explanation only after a failure or hide source state behind a
status label. Derived views do not silently replace authoritative records.
Acceptance example: from a result or refusal, a fresh reader follows the trace to
the source, applicable rule, actual check and recorded next action without chat memory.

## Compute outside the individual work context
Compute coordinates shared durable state, task contracts, dependencies, handoffs,
checks and recorded routes. Models receive focused inputs, ask for help and return
their work/learning. Their context loss must not erase the task or change its status.
Models supply intelligence; deterministic tools handle as much exact work as practical.

## Help and bounded intelligence
A request first uses a known operation or preserved answer where appropriate.
Where interpretation is needed, compute can dispatch a bounded intelligence task
under a recorded scope/resource grant. Qualify cheap models by task-specific tests:
correct answers, missing evidence, stale/conflicting sources, abstention and cost
of independent review. Preserve evidence, limits and failures. No model call is
authorized merely because this direction is recorded; execution needs its resource
and authority contract. Escalation follows a recorded route up to the Director.

## Rejection must follow explicit logical contracts
Director warning: be careful how compute rejects model work; kernel provides
consistent logical structure throughout the project. Compute must name the exact
applicable rule/version, input and observed failure when refusing an operation.
Mechanical contract failure, incomplete evidence, unknown capability, semantic
disagreement and lack of authority are different outcomes; do not collapse them.
Keep the submitted work and partial evidence, explain the correction or help route,
and hold only affected actions. Do not silently delete, rewrite or invent judgment.
An absent/contradictory rule is a finding/escalation, not permission to invent law.
Retries respond to diagnosed failure and recorded limits; repeated prompting is
not a way to manufacture the preferred answer. Compute's completeness checks do
not establish meaning, quality or Director approval.
Acceptance example: a rejected submission remains retrievable with the precise
failed check, governing source, preserved bytes and a recorded correction/escalation
path. Authorized content disagreements go to intelligence/authority, not an invented
format gate. Valid negative-test refusal and unexpected execution failure differ.

## Game/session handback
Record important state/events during a session. End-of-session handback reconciles
what became established, commitments, uncertainty, branches and continuation state.
A summary must not erase source events, player words or known disagreement.
Next workers receive focused context with deeper history available on demand.
