## 2. Fixed review shape and record states

See templates/review-record.md. Six short body sections:
1. Deliverable and goal alignment.
2. Verified facts, claims and verification limits.
3. Findings and unresolved decisions, including the two completion checks.
4. Learning worth preserving.
5. One recommended next bounded action.
6. Prime responses, Director replies and disposition history.

Header binds actor, request, target revision, source list, boot mode and status.
Finding rows bind an ID, kind, observation/evidence, consequence, proposed action,
pass lineage, Prime disposition and any decision reference. Observations, inferences
and proposals remain distinguishable. Missing evidence is reported as missing;
the Observer does not manufacture a test pass from a fluent return.

Proposed review-status meanings:
- open: request awaiting review, or a submitted review awaits complete Prime responses;
- answered: every finding has an explicit Prime response. A planned repair may
  still be pending verification; answered DOES NOT mean the output is correct;
- closed: Prime (within its grant) or Director records the final dispositions,
  evidence/decision links and reason for closing. Outstanding fixes cannot simply
  vanish: they must be verified or explicitly retained/deferred by the responsible
  authority with a follow-up job and reason. No automatic closure on a reply.

Also keep review_phase: requested/submitted and per-finding resolution:
unresolved/fix-planned/verified-fixed/answered-with-reason/director-pending/decided.
This distinguishes an empty intake record from an actual finding and a reply from
a completed fix. These fields are a draft data contract, not an implemented parser.
Observer may submit findings but may not close the review or mark a fix accepted.
Administrative review closure never grants release/approval to the underlying work.

## 3. Exact Prime discovery step

For an OBSERVED assignment, use this proposed loop:
1. Prime writes a named Observer request in the target batch's return flow before
   the review is wanted; request includes exact scope, sources and output location.
2. On the Builder return, manual intake (later Clerk) creates the requested empty
   record and links return <-> request <-> review; 0611 gets its pointer.
3. Observer is explicitly invoked by Director or Prime within delegation. It
   reads records and submits findings; it does not watch the filesystem continuously.
4. Prime reads the Builder return AND its requested Observer record/intake status
   BEFORE writing the next pack based on that return. Prime records responses.
5. Repairs, reasons or escalation become the next recorded action. The following
   review can verify that the disposition actually landed.

An empty/unsubmitted review is never a clean bill of health. Prime may draft ideas
while waiting, but must not represent the requested review as complete. A recorded
scoped waiver/escalation is possible; the Observer itself cannot veto work or
grant the waiver. We are proposing selected reviews first, not a mandatory review
of every file or an unbounded new gate on all work.

## 4. What Prime does with each finding

Exactly one current disposition, preserved with its history:
- fix in a named next batch/#NN; finding remains pending until verification;
- answer with a reason and evidence, including why the suggested change is not
  appropriate or why the observation is a context/capability gap;
- elevate to Director with the precise unresolved question and consequences.

The reply lands on the review record, with links to the affected subject page.
Page-local technical reasons stay on that subject; the Observer points to them.
Observer disagreement with a reply is retained through an additional observation
or the next requested review; Prime cannot erase it by rewriting the finding.
Closing a review documents a disposition, not universal truth. If authority is
insufficient or the consequence requires it, Director decides.

Proposed TWO-PASS rule for approval: count reviewed RETURNED REVISIONS for a stable
finding lineage, not chat messages, duplicate submissions or repeated reads. Initial
observed returned revision is pass 1. If the finding is still unresolved after the
next reviewed returned revision (pass 2), Prime elevates it before a third repair
pass. A promised next fix is not resolution. A documented reason resolves a finding
only when it actually answers the issue within the responsible authority's scope.
Urgent consequential problems can escalate immediately, without waiting for two.
Repeated findings across unrelated jobs are recurrence, not automatically passes
in this same finding chain. Do not elevate an entire project just because another
two batches have happened.

The Director's exact reply, source, target revision and interpretation land on
the record and link back to any original decision batch. If no reply exists, keep
director-pending. No model-written paraphrase becomes an execution release.

## 5. Clerk's future mechanical responsibilities — NOT BUILT

- Only for requested observed work: reserve a free record slot, construct the
  template, pin target/source identities and record requester/output scope.
- Link the return and record; index requested, open and answered-unclosed reviews.
- Refuse hash/identity mismatch, duplicate slot allocation, unauthorized field
  mutation and inconsistent state transitions. Repeated identical delivery is
  idempotent; altered contents under one identity require reconciliation.
- Validate required response/disposition fields, next-job/decision links and
  pass-counter lineage; produce a Director escalation obligation at the threshold.
- Count unique findings and review occurrences separately using recorded kind and
  finding IDs; do not infer that matching kinds have the same root cause.
- Derive queue/live views from records and preserve the result plus delivery
  obligation if an external recipient is unavailable.

Intelligence supplies kinds, adequacy, meaning, routes and decisions. Compute does
not decide that a why is good, that a model is wrong, or that a shared rule should
change. The current labels/status edits remain manual until a released tool slice
implements these checks. No claim of enforcement or new tools in this proposal.

## 6. Invocation and cold boot

Request fields: request ID; requester and authority/source; intended Observer;
target file AND commit/hash; question/scope; permitted evidence with exact revisions;
output review path; kind of boot; review criteria; any deliberately withheld source
and its reason; next recipient. Record request on the target batch and index it.
No constant polling. Repeated checks require an explicit new request or standing
delegation, neither created here.

Boot order: named role contract -> root entry/desk map at the declared revision ->
relevant kernel sources and their authority status -> request -> target/evidence ->
own review. Do not provide private explanatory chat or Prime's review conclusions
as substitutes for the source. If expected evidence is absent, report the gap and
where the attempt stopped. Distinguish an intentional documented withhold from
an accidentally missing source, and a personal uncertainty from a demonstrated
defect. The Observer is allowed to say cannot verify.

Record boot_mode honestly:
- fresh-session: new session receives only role/entry/request/source records;
- record-only-existing-session: a persistent bot is instructed to ground the review
  in records but may retain earlier context. Useful independence check, not proof
  of a genuinely cold model. Do not label it fresh merely because told to forget.

Completion has two distinct answers: functional evidence supports the output;
and a fresh Builder can reproduce it from seated instructions/dependencies. If the
instructions are still drafts, say so and assess draft reproducibility separately.
The Observer reads evidence; it does not execute code/tasks to manufacture missing
verification. It may request a separate authorized verification job. A successful
fresh process, cold model and new human Director are different kinds of evidence.

Preservation check: source sections have identities and retained/changed/moved/
merged/removed/new dispositions with reasons, destinations and authorizing batch/
#NN references. Compute checks actual correspondence where implemented; Observer
examines meaning. A hash proves byte identity, not adequate meaning or permission.
If the section inventory/authority record is missing, report coverage unknown;
never invent reasons or approvals to finish the form.
