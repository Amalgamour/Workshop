The bot says "one review page per return". Recommendation interprets that as a
review record on the SAME addressed work page. A new seqaddr page with the same
number but a different title would conflict with one-number/one-name.

not two messages or two arbitrary jobs. Repetition counts and semantic adequacy
are different. Prime may answer, but must preserve unclosed obligations/dissent.
"Reads only" means source work read-only; writing its own named review is the
explicit exception. No source repairs or code execution by Observer.
An existing bot's record-only read cannot prove a genuinely cold session. Record
boot_mode. Draft reproducibility and reproduction from seated instructions differ.
