## 2. Operator walkthrough (proposed; Prime is the operator)
1. **Trigger.** Prime decides the work is stuck or nearly ready for release testing, and names the question and the artifact cut. Ordinary Colab otherwise: stop here.
2. **Independent packet.**
   - Commit `evidence/reserved05.01.json` (stage `independent`).
   - Write an ordinary `colab-request/2` that:
     - lists the descriptor, the requirements and the authority limits under `required_sources`;
     - lists every builder-context path under `withheld`;
     - puts the `reserved-findings` block instructions in `method`.
   - `prepare` → `reserved_check.py launch`. Expect `launch-ready-as-declared`, with label `independent-fresh-declared` or `-freshness-unknown`.
3. **Launch** to a reviewer session Prime selects. The record says only what Prime knows: a model, family or session it doesn't know stays `unknown`.
4. **Seal.** Capture the return as today, `place-return` 05.01, then record `06.01.review.md` with route `request-next`, `next_pass: 05.02` and findings `[]`. The findings stay the reviewer's, undispositioned until reconciliation.
5. **Informed packet.**
   - Commit `05.01.return.md` (the exact placed bytes) and `evidence/reserved05.02.json` (stage `informed`, naming the member and return hash).
   - Write a request whose `required_sources` add the builder context, the sealed first return and the descriptor, with the `reserved-reconciliation` instructions in `method`.
   - `prepare` → `reserved_check.py stage`. Expect `informed-launch-ready-as-declared` and any recorded differences.
6. **Launch to the same reviewer, or another.** A change is recorded, not refused.
7. **Capture the 05.02 return** → `reserved_check.py reconcile --record …`. Expect `reconciled-as-declared` or `incomplete`.
8. **Prime writes `06.02.review.md`** with its ordinary dispositions and Director replies. The reserved record is evidence for that review, not its verdict.
