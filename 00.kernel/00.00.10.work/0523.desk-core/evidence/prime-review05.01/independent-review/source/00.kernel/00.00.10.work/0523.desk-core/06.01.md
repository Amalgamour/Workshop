## Binding
Task binding hashes title, body, expected outputs, scope, agreement, and the
declared evidence identities. It does not hash the return-pointer section or the
review section. Registering a claim, writing a pointer, and rendering a review
must leave that binding unchanged.
Review binding hashes the task binding, the receipt, the agreement hash, the
evidence hash, and the supplied review identity. Re-rendering the review or the
pointer does not stale it. Changing task body, bound evidence, or agreement does.
The previous review stays readable and is not current authority when stale.

## Authority
Task, release, claim, receipt, and review JSON records are the records.
Faces are projections. If a projection disagrees with its record, the view
reports the conflict and does not pick a newest file as authority.
There is no second job ledger. Pending work is derived from the book.
Exact page/pass reads use the computed path. They do not scan the live history.
