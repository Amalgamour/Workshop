date: 2026-11-02
source: Director agreement 0412.prime-review/06.07 #03 and #06
state: planned after core-managed review is used by Prime; no cleanup or archive changes authorized

Goal: keep the full evidence retrievable while compute presents a small review packet. File count and hashing are compute work, not hundreds of rows the model must read by default.

Reuse existing manifests and canonical review records. Produce summary: selected run/input/return identities, file/byte counts, executed checks and actual outcomes, first failures, independent versus author cases, open holds, integrity mismatches and exact drill-down paths. A matching hash is byte evidence, not semantic success or a guarantee that an absent archive remains available.

Retain raw first failures, one or two representative successful runs, generation code/runtime/seed/config and complete index. Other full fixtures may use a separately selected durable archive, with verified retrieval and backup; D:/librarian/temp alone is not durable retention. No existing sealed evidence is deleted or rewritten. Only future authoring practices and a separately reviewed migration may change physical placement.

Initial version can keep file-row manifests unchanged and summarize them mechanically. Tree rows are optional later work requiring an explicit schema and verifier contract (deterministic paths, link handling, identity and retrieval); do not insert folder rows into the present file-only schema and claim verification. No generic evidence warehouse/database is required for the first slice.

Finish: reproduce a real review's outcomes from its summary plus selected drill-down; prove raw failures and omitted-from-default-context material can be retrieved; report missing/stale archive without success. Compare source/evidence identities with the original corpus. One small counterexample where a summary overstates a not-run check must be rejected. Run only after the core review loop works for Prime, in its own bounded pack.
