## Job

owner: kernel-only

Face 02 supplies a working form of a named subject section. A form is a card, a template, a schema, or a compute form. A card is read for a quick use. A template builds a new artifact. A schema checks an artifact. A compute form is code or configuration that a declared runner loads. Each form names its kind. The kinds are not interchangeable: a card is not a template, and a schema is not permission. A form does not add normative conditions absent from face 01.

## Coverage

owner: kernel-only

Create a face 02 section when a working form serves the job. Its source section is explicit. The face 02 section heading is the exact face 01 heading it represents. Labels inside the form are its content, not section keys. A card that is shorter than the rule stays. A card whose only extra sentence is to read the source comes off. A template or schema may be longer than the rule, when it carries structure the rule does not need to state. If no form is needed, its face is absent and the menu omits it.

## Agreement

owner: kernel-only

Preserve the governing conditions, exceptions, and limits in the compact form. A card that changes a condition is a conflict even when its heading matches. Report that conflict with both source cuts. Read the actual governing file before a consequential write. Governing conditions, exceptions, and limits outrank shortness. A card may be shorter. A shorter card that drops a limit or adds a condition is a conflict. Shortness does not win. A template, schema, or compute form is held to the same test.

## Requirements

owner: kernel-only

A form may require a field, a shape, an order, a count, or an encoding. Each such requirement carries one of two grounds. It represents a condition its source already states; or it is the form's own structure, which changes how the artifact is written and not what it means. Test a requirement this way: if an artifact that fails it could carry the same meaning in another representation, the requirement is structure. If failing it changes what is required, allowed, or forbidden, it is a condition. A condition belongs on face 01 of the page that owns the subject and passes that page's admission test. Calling it validation does not admit it. A requirement whose ground is not stated is a finding, not a check.

## Fields

owner: kernel-only

A template declares each field: its name and job, its shape, and whether it is required, optional, or may be declared not applicable. It also declares who supplies the field. Intelligence supplies meaning. Compute derives structure: identity, numbering, paths, layout, dates of record, hashes, and encoding. A declared source supplies a retrieved value. Absent, empty, and not applicable are different answers where the form says so. Compute does not invent a missing meaning; it asks for it. An example value shows shape. It is not a decision and not a default. A repeating block exists only where the form declares it, with the identity scheme compute assigns to its items. A form that declares no repeating block has none.

## Sources

owner: kernel-only

A form names its page, face, section, kind, and version. A use binds the exact bytes of the form and of each source it declares. Retrieve a form by its address, not by the newest filename and not by proximity. A copy names the form it was taken from; if that form has changed, the copy is stale and is not used. A form may declare what it depends on: another form, a source, a module, a resource, or an environment. Compute resolves the declared dependencies and reports any that are missing, stale, ambiguous, or conditional. It does not infer an undeclared dependency, and a hash does not prove compatibility. A relationship between pages is recorded on face 08.

## Construction

owner: kernel-only

Compute builds the whole artifact from the form and the supplied meaning, and shows the whole artifact for review. Text is read and written in the encoding the form declares, never in a platform default. A failed check names the field, the expected and the observed shape, keeps the supplied input, and gives the correction route. Nothing supplied is silently dropped, re-encoded, or substituted. A refused input is not a rejected idea. A changed form, source, dependency, or supplied value makes the earlier review stale. Compute checks the declared structure; intelligence judges meaning. Building, checking, and review do not approve, seat, lock, or release. Those stay with their own laws.
