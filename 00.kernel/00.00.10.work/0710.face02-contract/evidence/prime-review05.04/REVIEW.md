## P3 - Make acceptance state-specific rather than impossible
SEATING-TOOL-SPEC finish item 2 says every held/refused outcome writes nothing. A multi-face operation can legitimately complete face one before face two fails and the release becomes held half-seated. The intended invariant must distinguish validation refusal (zero operation writes), an intentional or failed partial execution (record exact writes and hold), and a recovery refusal (no additional mutation). No full-transaction guarantee exists. Preserve partial state and journal evidence; do not delete completed work to satisfy a no-write assertion. Also specify that partial/journal writes and raw-image creation are counted in reported changes. Current no-write fixture checks are useful within their tested call boundaries, not a universal atomicity proof.
