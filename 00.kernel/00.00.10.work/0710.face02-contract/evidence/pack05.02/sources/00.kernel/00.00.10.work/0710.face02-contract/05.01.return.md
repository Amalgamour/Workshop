- **Sources** (new): identity, exact-byte binding, retrieval by address, stale copies, declared dependencies, no undeclared inference, relationships on 08.
- **Construction** (new): the whole-artifact build and review, an explicit encoding, the failure content, nothing dropped or substituted, staleness, and no approval, seating, locking or release.

**It contains no pack, `#NN`, JSON, Python, filename or lifecycle law.**
