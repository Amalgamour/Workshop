## Method (fixed before drafting)
1. **A new `design05.04/lifecycle/` successor:** `lifecycle2.template.json`, `LIFECYCLE2.md`, `lifecycle_sim2.py`. The 05.03 files stay as they are.
2. **Raw before-images.** Stage, sign, approve and seat each keep the exact bytes they replace, under `records/images/<sha>.bin`. Rollback restores from those bytes; nothing is rebuilt from fixture strings.
3. **Journal** entries carry the planned and expected hashes. Every restart, including one at a completed J5, re-reads the files and checks them against the journal. Unknown, malformed or partial records are findings and hold.
4. **Ledger** lines are typed: `planned`, `retired`, `rolled-back`. History is never rewritten.
5. **Rollback:**
   - needs a SYNTHETIC fixture Director decision naming the seated, retired and target hashes;
   - runs a full preflight before the first rename;
   - is journaled R1–R5 and restartable;
   - **never overwrites** an occupied or changed destination.
6. **Cases:**
   - the 13 from 05.03, re-expressed for the successor;
   - Prime's two probes exactly (completed-restart drift; changed-seat rollback);
   - changed retired file, missing before-image, missing or wrong decision, occupied destination;
   - interrupted rollback with restart;
   - a crash at each seat step J1–J5 with restart;
   - a malformed journal.
   
   **One run.**
7. **Then:** the template, the update draft and a seating-tool specification aligned to the behaviour actually shown.
