# Preservation rationale - possible future learning corpus
date: 2026-11-03
who: Codex Prime
state: Director intent captured; proposed future data practices, no training/export release

Source: 0416.desk-app/06.12.md preserves the Director's exact statement and the external inspiration evidence. The Director sees accumulated packs and returns from varied models and repositories as a potentially valuable future model-training resource. Protecting and recovering this work is a product priority.

Prime interpretation: preserve the authentic work first, including unsuccessful attempts. A useful future learning unit should be able to recover the instruction/design context and dependencies, exact input revision, worker/model identity where actually known, original output, checks and independent critiques, correction and reason, Director's verbatim intervention, disposition and evidenced final outcome. Unknown identity/outcome stays unknown. Keep model interpretation distinct from Director speech.

An approved result is not automatically a correctness label, and an author test is not an independent check. Repeated copies and retries must not silently become many independent successes. Bind records across book instances using address plus source/revision identity. Preserve raw evidence separately from any selected/compressed training projection so selection can be audited and reversed.

Possible future tooling: provenance inventory, permission/export metadata, duplicate/lineage detection, outcome labels grounded in checks, and a reproducible dataset export with source links. These are candidates for later design, not permission to upload, train, delete history or change the current archive. Heterogeneous models and large volume may help breadth, but training benefit remains a hypothesis to test. Neither uniqueness of our corpus nor a training performance gain is established by this note.
