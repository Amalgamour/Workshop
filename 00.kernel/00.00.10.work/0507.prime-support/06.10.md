## Choices (routine)
C01. Inspector path: the sibling `return_inspect.py` next to the requested `tools.return_capture`, the same way clerk_adapter is found next to prime_support. The request schema is unchanged, and no new path field or version discovery is added. Both files are hash-checked before every launch.
C02. Stage 08 runs the inspector once per requested member. Its exact stdout bytes go to `08.member-NN.inspect.raw`. The observation records:
- the observation time, the tool, argv and exit;
- stderr, the stdout hash and its byte count;
- a source kind of "live member directory via the pinned inspector", not a commit blob.
The old `verify` call and its human-readable output are dropped. The inspector already uses the helper's `verify_member`.
C03. Interface checks only, not a package validator. The observation fails, as an explicit failed observation, when any of these hold:
- the stdout is not strict JSON, or is not an object;
- the schema is unknown;
- the status is not one of the five, or the exit does not match the status;
- `member` is not the requested name;
- `problems` is not a list of strings, or is empty for anything but complete (and the reverse);
- `capture` is present with a status other than complete, or absent with complete;
- stderr is not empty.
The identity fields this lab reads are type-checked before use: `submission_id`, the reservation, manifest and receipt hashes, and the return sha and bytes.
C04. Readiness: a requested member is ready only when its observation is ok and its status is complete.
- Anything else makes prepare `partial`, and gets a remediation line recorded in 08 and in 19.
- Capture identities are only those the inspector returned for complete; there are none otherwise.
- `scope` says custody only: review, acceptance and publication elsewhere are not inferred either way.
- An empty member list stays valid.
C05. The packet holds each member observation's exact stdout text as a ref:
- `kind: live-inspection`, `path: member:<name>`, with no commit;
- placed after the four required commit refs and before `packet[]`;
- counted against the same 12-ref and 128 KiB caps; overflow is an omission record and makes prepare partial.
The full bytes stay in the raw file. The O1 scope counts only commit-blob paths plus the status scope, so observation refs never become work targets.
C06. Stability: stage 10 re-reads the tool hashes. If they still match, it re-inspects every member into `10.member-NN.inspect.raw` and compares the identity tuple: status, reservation, submission, manifest, receipt and return. A change adds `members` to `changed`, so the attempt becomes unstable and partial. If a tool hash changed, re-inspection is skipped and the stage records why. Two reads are two observations; no atomic snapshot is claimed.
C07. test_17 changes to the new contract, with positive and negative assertions. The demo is unchanged.
