## My understanding of the intended Prime loop
1. **Prime records a bounded task** as an N5NN batch pass (`NNNN.subject/05.NN.md`), with a frozen source packet. The desk-wide selector 0401/06.01 stays a record that views project and check (R3). It is not demoted.
2. **The Director releases the work in words.** The words are relayed and recorded. A worker claims. Neither is authentication.
3. **The worker returns.** Compute seals the return and its evidence on the matching return line: N5NN maps to N1NN with the same N, NN and subject, and each pass is a tail on that line (0417/06.01). N7NN Colab stays page-local with `05.NN.pack.md`, `05.NN.return.md` and `06.NN.review.md`. Old sequential `01NN` members must be migrated without relabelling sealed /1 bytes. A receipt is custody only.
4. **Prime retrieves** the exact task, return and evidence by page and pass (bounded, R6). The model supplies typed judgment. Compute renders the full readable review on the source batch under `## Review — Codex Prime`, or on the N7NN `06.NN.review.md` tail (R2). The typed record and raw checks can stay as evidence.
5. **Review binding survives mechanical rendering but goes stale** when the task, its source address, the evidence, the receipt or the agreement changes.
6. **After restart,** the pending state, next actor and next action, plus unresolved Director questions, come from records alone.
7. **Inspectability** (0416/01.003): from any success or refusal, a fresh reader reaches the task, source versions, governing rule, observed check, model judgment, changes, decision and next route. A mechanical refusal, incomplete evidence, unknown capability, semantic disagreement and missing authority are distinct outcomes. Submitted work is never lost.

## Boundaries
- **Director:** releases, approves, decides authority and design forks, and answers held questions.
- **Prime:** prepares packs, reviews, supplies judgment, owns the shared facade integration (R4) and the selector.
- **Workers:** build in their lanes, return work and evidence, and do not accept their own work.
- **Compute:** routes, constructs, seals, checks identities and shape, persists transitions, and refuses with a reason. It never judges meaning, approves or invents law.
