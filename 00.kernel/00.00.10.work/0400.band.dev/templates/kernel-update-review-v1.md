# Critical kernel update review - template v1
date: 2026-11-02
owner: Director-adopted workshop dev process, 0400.band.dev/06.01
state: operational development agreement; no kernel seating or runtime enforcement claim
template-id: kernel-update-review/1

Use for every future critical kernel update in this workshop. A work page records an instance of the form; the template stays reusable. Bind explicit source/template revisions. Never select a governing revision by newest filename alone. Keep machine declaration kernel-update-review-v1.json consistent; contradictions go to the Director for the affected act.

## #01 - Establish the change set
Record: purpose/problem, book instance, selected seats and candidates, exact versions/hash/bytes, governing source sections, linked dev/work/Colab pages, authority/scope, affected dependents and faces that must change together. Compute measures; intelligence proposes the group. Output: bounded change-set record. Missing or conflicting source is exposed. For the current work, 0001 and 0002 are one change set because their correspondence rules must agree. This does not approve their exact bytes.

## #02 - Account for existing meaning
One row per source section (and explicit new sections): source heading, disposition retained/changed/moved/merged/removed/new, candidate destination, reason, authorizing work item/Director decision and affected connections. Preserve conditions, exceptions and limits. A removed or merged section names where its useful meaning lands or why removal is authorized. No unexplained disappearance. Output: reviewable preservation/impact map; no automatic semantic-equivalence claim from a hash.

## #03 - Director review in bounded pieces
Prime presents one page or manageable section group. Each #NN contains: current requirement/source cut; proposed text; concrete difference and why; example behavior; important conflict/open choice; Prime recommendation. Director answers with #NN. Keep exact words and attribution separately from Prime interpretation, disposition and successor review. Resolve affected choices before exact approval; hold dependent action without freezing unrelated work. Output: attributed answers and disposition for every finding. A model never chooses a convenient source in a law/form conflict.

## #04 - Revise and demonstrate
Notes first; candidate from notes; reread fully, add notes and integrate on the second pass. Revise a successor, preserve earlier returned bytes and update the map. Compute checks identity, shape, citations/links, coverage and declared tests; intelligence checks meaning. Examples show intended behavior, failures and recovery at the applicable scope. Preserve original failed tests separately from successors. Use a bounded fresh Colab when stuck or ready for testing before release; not on every minor edit. Record prior involvement, agreed/disagreed findings and dispositions. Output: revised candidate and evidence, with limitations exposed.

## #05 - Sign and approve exact replacements
Model requests signing; compute verifies the declared checks and records signed identity. Prime brings the final summary, preservation map, unresolved limitations and exact signed candidates to the Director. Approval of direction is not approval of bytes. Director approves exact candidate identities with attributed words; record content identity and allowed mechanical stamp transitions separately. Later content change requires renewed applicable review and approval. Output: sign/check and Director approval records, or a visible hold. No model approval inferred from tests or filename.

## #06 - Seat through the verified lifecycle
Use only the reviewed procedure/tool under an explicit execution release. Recheck current predecessor, approved candidate, chosen warehouse layout/destinations, record roots and authority. Journal, preserve raw predecessors and qualified originating identity, seat the replacements and verify actual results. A declared multi-face release may be partially interrupted; it remains visibly incomplete/held until authorized recovery. Restart verifies bytes even after completion. Rollback requires applicable Director judgment and preflight, preserves both versions and history. No manual-copy shortcut or claim that the simulator is the seating tool. Lock and promotion are separate authorized acts. Output: verified completion or preserved partial/held evidence.

## #07 - Preserve the reusable result
Link decisions, source comparison, findings/dispositions, tests, signed approvals and execution/recovery records from the work page. After completion, consolidate the strongest reproducible execution instructions into the reusable seated 05 under its applicable lifecycle. Preserve the development history and Director contributions; consolidation does not authorize deleting them. Record remaining issues and exact dependency/version applicability so a later worker can reproduce the result. Output: recoverable history plus reusable instructions, not closure from a green test alone.

## Instance record - minimum fields
- Change-set identity, selected book/source/template revisions and work references.
- Section map and review #NN items.
- Director verbatim answers; separate Prime interpretations and dispositions.
- Candidate revision history, demonstrations, findings and Colab trigger/result.
- Signed candidate/check identities and exact Director approval record.
- Selected verified seating path, execution authority, before/after/partial/recovery records.
- Reusable 05 destination, preserved history links and completion/remaining holds.

Each stage records actual state (not-started/in-progress/held/complete), evidence and next action. Missing evidence is not completion. Compute-owned identity and state handling does not decide meaning. Routine mechanical failures get a correction route; judgment conflicts come to the Director. The core-managed review entry is being built separately; no tool activation is implied by this form.
