# Director clarification - every review has a Director-facing landing
date: 2026-10-31
who: Codex Prime
state: Director instruction recorded; not a solo-task release

Source: ../0704.desk-core-integration/06.02.notes.md, #02 verbatim reply.

## Normal operation
When Prime finishes a review, bring the result to the Director: what was checked,
what it establishes, findings and limits, alignment and recommended next step.
Keep the existing short substantive reporting floor for routine work. A review
must not disappear into files or silent model-to-model handoffs. The record and
evidence remain on the proper workflow page as well as being reported to the person.

## Explicit solo-task operation
When the Director explicitly delegates a bounded solo task, Prime may coordinate
workers and internal review/repair cycles through compute within that grant.
Do not infer solo authority from builder availability or a hypothetical example.
The final report always reaches the Director with deliverables, evidence, why,
remaining limits and unresolved matters. Internal reviews remain durably recorded.
Any judgment outside the delegated scope is elevated before affected work proceeds;
the solo mode does not authorize Prime to choose the Director's tradeoffs silently.

## Reporting, approval and trace
A completed review report informs the Director; an approval request asks for a
decision required by the recorded scope. Do not add an approval gate for routine
technical correction solely because a report must be shown. Neither silence nor
report delivery supplies approval. Record the mode and scope, review status,
whether the Director-facing report has been delivered, and any decision pending.
Creating a file alone is not proof the Director has seen it. Do not claim read
receipt or acknowledgment from delivery. Final findings are never silently omitted.
