## 4. Factfind (opinion lives here only)
- **The loop's division of labour is right.** Prime supplies words; compute binds, measures, refuses and renders. What makes it costly today is that the template stops at keys. The cheapest relief is in compute and docs, not in Prime's discipline.
- **F4 and F8 matter more than F1–F3** for actual use:
  - F4 means a `held` real review would read like any other on the page.
  - F8 means Prime can lose a judgment's worth of work to a conflict that was knowable at prepare.
- **The B and C results are good news.** Recovery from an interrupted render is a plain `recover`, and repeating it is harmless.

## 8. Learning
Two things are reported as one in today's output: "the mechanism completed" (`full_success`) and "what the reviewer decided" (disposition). That is the same split that `accepted: false` guards at the app level, and it is worth carrying onto the page (F4). The pilot's sample used `disposition: accepted` while every flag said `accepted: false`, and nothing on the page shows which is which.
