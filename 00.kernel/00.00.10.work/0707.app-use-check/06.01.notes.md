- **Write limits.** Writes go only to disposable books under my scratchpad (outside the checkout), to `colab/labs/0707.app-use-check/pass01`, and to `0707/evidence/build05.01`.
- **Unexpected-write check.** `git status --porcelain` is taken before and after, and must differ only by my allowed paths. The live 0182 member and the 0519/05.01 page are fingerprinted, and `return-line-layout.json` must stay absent.
- **Material.** The 0182 member is copied from the **pack snapshot** bytes, and the copy must equal the pins. The conflict page is the pack snapshot of 0519/05.01 (b72c8aa0…).
- **Synthetic target.** The success book gets a new page at the path derived from the descriptor's origin (`<page>/<batch>.md` = `0519.desk-app/05.01.md`). Its content is labelled SYNTHETIC PENDING, with outside and tail tokens.

I write my own `assignment.txt` and `note.md` with labelled synthetic text, so the D1 question (O4) is tested rather than masked.
