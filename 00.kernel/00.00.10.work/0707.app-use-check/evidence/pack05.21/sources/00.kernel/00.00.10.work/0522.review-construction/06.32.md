## Grammar chosen for the successor

Recognized declaration labels are `Returned:`, `Submission:`, `Artifact manifest:`, and `Verification:`. A line is recognized when the stripped line starts with one of those labels. Blank lines and `Invocation/claim:` stay skipped. Any other line, including a Notes line, stays the diagnostic `unparsed-return-line`. It does not become a declaration and it does not rewrite a filename.

Accepted N7 forms, unchanged from pass04, are:

- `Returned: PATH (ATTRIBUTION), sha256 H, N bytes.` with a descriptive attribution. This binds `declared_form` `n7-returned`.
- `Artifact manifest: PATH, sha256 H, N bytes.` with PATH limited to the pass04 path class. This binds `declared_form` `n7-manifest`.
- The pass03 counted form whose tail is empty, ` Excludes itself.`, or ` Excludes itself and this page.`
- The pass03 field form when `suffix_ok` accepts the tail.

An attribution that fullmatches and contains a 64-hex digest or a recognized label is `attribution-not-descriptive` and `malformed-n7-declaration`. The intake state is `blocked`. A recognized line that fullmatches no accepted form is `unparsed-return-line` and `malformed-n7-declaration`, and the state is `blocked`. An `Artifact manifest:` line that contains the N7 delimiter `, sha256 ` and fails the exact N7 manifest form is the same block. That covers the A-01 trailing note, a missing period, and the double comma `m.json,,`. The field form is not applied to that line, so the filename does not become `m.json,` or `m.json,,`.

A blocked parse clears every field bound from that pointer. A valid line before or after the malformed line does not remain bound. `submission-not-declared` is left for pointers that are not blocked. Duplicate and cross-form valid lines keep `ambiguous-submission` and `ambiguous-manifest`, bind nothing, and stay unblocked. Empty, absent, and `PENDING.` stay their current states.

Labels with no value (`Returned:`, `Submission:`, `Artifact manifest:`, `Verification:`) are recognized and blocked. A pass03 field line whose filename contains a comma, semicolon, or closing parenthesis stays accepted in N7 context. `Submission: return.md, sha256` still binds `return.md,`. `Artifact manifest: m.json sha256` still binds `m.json`. Those lines do not use a failed N7 comma-delimiter form.

N5NN and an empty kind call the captured pass03 parser object. The double comma on N5 still binds `m.json,,`. The A-01 suffix on N5 still binds `m.json,`. This pack does not add a global filename ban.
