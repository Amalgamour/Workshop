## #03 — Failure of failure reporting
Failure-log creation/write/readback may itself raise OSError or a controlled refusal.
Catch those inside the final failure path. Preserve the original handoff reason,
report the secondary log error on stdout, keep any partial log bytes, and return
controlled exit2 without traceback. Never overwrite an existing log or complete a
handoff during error reporting. A logging failure does not erase prior writes or
convert an incomplete capture into success. No broad catch that hides programming
errors as successful outcomes. Keep strict normal binding/path/pin safeguards.
