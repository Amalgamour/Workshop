## Work planned
`evidence/flow05.19/CONTRACT.md` states the expected outcomes before code. The new module is `labs/capture-flow-rehearsal-pass01`. It is a standard-library harness. It is not an installed app command, a dispatcher, or a new lifecycle.

Explicit fixture setup, outside the checkout and outside Git, stands a disposable context, an external Clerk store, the design instruction and claim, a link, a pre-reserved book member, and the protocol worker. Queue and claim are setup calls on the pinned Clerk. Delivery goes through the accepted `clerk_seam.perform`. One real protocol-worker subprocess is allowed on the success path. Collection returns the job through the seam. The worker's `result.json` is then the SYNTHETIC final-report payload. Its bytes are preserved. It is not a narrative builder report and not a semantic review. Transport evidence stays a separate field.

Handoff is the accepted `perform(binding_path)` from the source-view, with the private `hook` seam for the post-capture stop. The CLI of the pinned tools gains no fault switch. The rehearsal does not call queue, claim, return, or reserve from inside handoff. It does not allocate a live member, mutate `0146` during the fixture, or touch the live kernel except for this batch's own notes, evidence, and return page.

The bounded checks are: one complete flow; the same delivery and handoff again with no second worker launch, no second returned event, and no changed source, job, member, or handoff-record bytes; a changed event binding and a changed report payload, each refused without another worker and without replacing the capture; a crashed worker with no capture; a stop after capture that keeps the member files, separates current custody from handoff completion, and treats the next call as attention rather than a resume.

`work_ref` and the fixture routing labels are supplied labels. A verified receipt is custody. It is not review, acceptance, acknowledgement, closure, or publication. Unknown pin, timeout, and inspection outcomes stay unknown. Recovery and a new submission are out of scope.

## Limits
The same-user gap between a hash read and a later write remains open. This is not an operating-system sandbox. The observation is one disposable fixture, not a general exactly-once guarantee. This rehearsal does not create a file symlink; WinError 1314 remains a platform limit and is not re-tested here. No automatic repair, cleanup, or allocation runs around a partial. Cleanup removes only a verified owned absolute temporary root, and it unlinks junctions with `os.rmdir` before `shutil.rmtree`.
