claim strict real-time guarantees or process-tree confinement.
Add a new no-stdin-reader fixture. A sufficiently large VALID envelope must not
block the adapter beyond the deadline plus a small documented cleanup allowance.

the suite. Verify receipt/diagnostics and no second launch on retry. Re-run small

result: DONE. bridge/adapter.py `_execute`: the stdin write now runs in its own thread (`_write_stdin`), so ONE deadline, t0 + timeout_s, covers writing stdin, reading stdout and stderr, and waiting for exit. result: DONE. New bridge/ids.py: `check()` and `store_dir()`, one identity and containment validator. deliver (through the envelope), status and recover all get their per-delivery folder only from `store_dir`; the CLI goes through them. It checks the identifier rules before any file is touched, then refuses a link or reparse point at the delivery folder, and confirms that the folder resolves directly inside the store.
