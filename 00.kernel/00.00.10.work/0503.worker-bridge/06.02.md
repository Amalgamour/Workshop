P01. BR-01 (the stdin write blocked the deadline). Cause: proc.stdin.write ran synchronously before the watchdog loop, so a child that never read its stdin held the adapter until the child left on its own. Correction: a writer thread for stdin, one deadline over all three streams and the wait, then a stated 2.0 s cleanup allowance, recorded per attempt. Evidence: the probe on the reviewed snapshot gave 3.048 s at a 0.2 s timeout; corrected, 0.231 s; the regression finished in about 0.34 s against a documented bound of 3.8 s. Lands: bridge/adapter.py (_execute, _write_stdin, CLEANUP_ALLOWANCE_S); CONTRACT.md R8.

P02. BR-02 (control-plane paths). Cause: status and recover joined an unchecked delivery_id to the store; only deliver validated it, through the envelope. Correction: bridge/ids.py, one check plus resolved containment, used by every entry and so by the CLI. The id rules became stricter than pass 01's: lowercase only (no case aliasing on Windows), a letter or digit at both ends (no trailing dot or space), no '..', no reserved device names. Evidence: probes before/after; 24 bad strings and 4 bad types refused through the library and the CLI; a junction inside the store refused; the outside sentinel unchanged. Lands: CONTRACT.md "Delivery identity" and R9.

P03. BR-03 (limits). Cause: an isinstance check on (int, float) admits bool, inf and NaN, and byte caps were never required to be integers. Correction: check_limits, finite and typed with lab ceilings (3600 s, 64 MiB), refused before launch; strict JSON for messages (no NaN, Infinity or duplicate keys); canonical() refuses NaN and Infinity. Evidence: 20 single-field cases, plus all-bools and a missing key, refused through deliver; the boundary values accepted. Lands: bridge/envelope.py, bridge/canon.py; CONTRACT.md R10 and R11.
