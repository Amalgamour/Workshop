# Notes — worker bridge, pass 03 (batch 05.03)
Date: 2026-10-26
Recorder: claude-opus-5-5, Colab
State: working notes for 05.03; evidence and proposals, not app/runtime claims.

Q01. BR-01b cause, confirmed. The reader threads were blocked in BufferedReader.read() on pipes a descendant had inherited, holding each stream's internal lock. After the bounded joins, pass 02's main thread called close() on those same streams, and close waited for the lock until the descendant exited. Marking cleanup.complete false did not bound the return. Reproduced on the pre-fix snapshot by the new regression (5.142 s and 5.160 s against a 3.7 s bound) and by the Prime's own worker and driver (5.087 s).

Q02. Correction: ownership, not force. Pipes are unbuffered (bufsize=0). Each stream has one owner thread that reads or writes and then closes it. The adapter freezes the captures with snapshot() and returns; it never closes, joins or waits on an owned stream after the allowance. No stream is force-closed from another thread, which would only move the same wait. The stdin writer loops over partial writes, because unbuffered writes may be short.

Q03. Lifecycle of a retained thread, observed. It is named in cleanup.retained, keeps draining its pipe (so the descendant is not blocked by a full pipe), drops late bytes, and closes its own handle when the descendant closes the other end. In the regressions the threads finished at 5.135 s and 5.184 s, when the 5-second descendant exited. If the descendant never exits, the thread and handle live until this interpreter exits (daemon thread). That is a retained resource of the calling process, reported, not reaped. Fine for a lab adapter; a long-running service would need a reaper or process-tree control, which is out of scope.

Q04. No promotion. The new kind incomplete-output is used when the child exits but its output is not at EOF within the budget. The partial snapshot is diagnostics only and is never parsed, even when the child had already written a complete result before starting the descendant (test_complete_result_followed_by_descendant_is_not_promoted). This is conservative: a real CLI that leaves a background helper holding stdout would be reported as incomplete, not collected.

Q05. Compatibility. "Delivered" now also requires both streams at EOF within the deadline (R15). The 28 earlier tests are unchanged and pass. The BR-01, BR-02 and BR-03 corrections are re-verified by tests/probes_pass02.py. Ordinary work closes cleanly (cleanup complete, nothing retained; demo-run-02-pass03).

Q06. No leftover processes. Every descendant self-terminates (5 s). A process listing after the runs found no fixture process (evidence/pass03/leftover-processes.txt).
