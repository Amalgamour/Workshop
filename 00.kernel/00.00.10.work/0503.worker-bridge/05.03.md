## #02 — Make cleanup return bounded without a blocking close
Remove the synchronous close/wait path that can wait on a reader's buffered-stream
lock after the cleanup budget. Establish clear stream/thread ownership and bounded
cleanup behavior. Do not force-close a stream in a way that simply moves the same
blocking wait elsewhere. Preserve bounded capture, finite timeout, uncertain
outcome, direct-child-only termination and idempotent retry behavior.

It is acceptable to report incomplete cleanup and retained background resources
honestly; document lifecycle/reaping limits. It is NOT acceptable to claim a
bounded adapter return while waiting for an unbounded descendant to close a pipe.
This does not require killing descendants or building an OS sandbox. Partial/late
output must not be promoted to a verified completed result after the deadline.
Snapshot final receipt evidence consistently and state what was not collected.
result: DONE. bridge/adapter.py `_execute` (CONTRACT.md R13-R15):
- Stream ownership: pipes are unbuffered (bufsize=0), and each stream has exactly one owner thread (`_reader` for stdout and stderr; `_write_stdin`, which now loops over partial writes) that is the only code to read, write or close it.
- The synchronous close after the joins is removed. After the deadline plus the 2.0 s allowance, `deliver()` freezes the captures (`_Capture.snapshot()`) and returns. It never closes, joins or waits on a stream a thread still owns, and nothing is force-closed from another thread.
- A thread still running is named in `cleanup.retained`, with a note. It keeps draining its pipe and dropping late bytes (counted, never added to the result), and closes its own handle when the descendant closes the other end, or when the interpreter exits (daemon thread).
- New transport kind `incomplete-output`: the child exited, but its output was not at EOF within the budget. The outcome is uncertain, collection is not attempted, and the partial snapshot is kept as diagnostics and never parsed, even when a complete result was written before the descendant started.
- "delivered" now requires both streams at EOF within the deadline.
- Preserved: bounded captures, the finite timeout, the uncertain outcome on timeout and output-limit, direct-child-only termination, and idempotent replay. No descendant is killed, and no process-tree manager or sandbox was added.
After: the new regression returns in 2.222 s and 2.220 s (retained: stdout reader, stderr reader; those threads finished at 5.135 s and 5.184 s, when the descendant exited; the retry was replayed in 0.008 s with 1 launch). The Prime's worker and driver against the corrected lab: 2.219 s, transport incomplete-output, cleanup.complete false, retained stdout and stderr readers (evidence/pass03/probes/prime-inherited-after.json).
