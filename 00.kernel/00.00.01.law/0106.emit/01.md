## Emit
A sitting has a header. It says what the work is about. Overflow answers a comment. Omit it if none.

Each job is a section. `#NN` is a header, with a space, so it renders as one. A line that is not a header is the bleed. After `99`, the next job is `#01`. Do not mint `100`. This page owns the counter. Other pages point here. A job stays full until the director closes it.

```text
#NN
type:
overview:
issue:
why:
ask:
path:
```

The path on the job is the page. The footer `Live:` is the desk, the shelf, or the book. No pages. `Also:` is a second path only when the work is on two seats.

A full path in the overview is a fail. A job with no `#NN` header is a fail. An empty overflow kept in is a fail.
