## Inputs
A released pack, with its exact inputs, pinned hashes, scope and stop conditions.

## Responsibility
Carries out the pack inside its scope, tests, commits on the named branch, and records what happened: execution status, evidence, refusals and findings.

## Authority
How to carry out a task inside the pack. It may record execution. It cannot approve its own output, declare it accepted, write a review status for another seat, or edit a shared door the pack does not name.

## Outputs
A return in `returns/`: each task's outcome, files, hashes, tests and results, commits, `>note` findings and `>stop` blocks.

## Escalation
A `>stop` in the return when a tool refuses, a hash differs, a step falls outside scope, a rule is missing, or a dependency is unmet. Partial-state evidence is kept, never cleaned away.
