## Route
A route is declared by intelligence (the Prime, or the Director) before work moves: from which seat, to which seat, on what condition. The Clerk does not invent a route and does not choose a recipient on its own judgment.

## Record
Every handoff is a recorded transition, not a message. The sender hands the Clerk the item, the result, and an exact artifact reference (path plus SHA-256). The Clerk validates the transition against the declared route and records it. A transition that is not on the route is refused.

## Review
A review covers one revision. It names the artifact hash it reviewed. If the artifact changes before or after the claim, the review return is refused. A review never migrates to changed work.

## Identity
Each delivery has an identity. Resubmitting the same bytes under the same identity changes nothing (idempotent). Different bytes under the same identity are refused.

## Wait
A dependency is explicit: an item names what it waits on. The wait states are written, never inferred:
- waiting on a dependency;
- waiting on a decision;
- waiting on a recipient;
- ready.

## Durable
A completed result and the obligation to deliver it next are recorded together, durably, before any external delivery is tried. If the recipient is unavailable, or the delivery fails, the result is kept and the obligation stays open. A failed delivery never loses a completed result and never counts as delivered.

## Not
Not the storage format: the lab's SQLite is evidence, not adopted (`labs/clerk-handoff-pass01`). Not the role definitions (`0062`–`0065`). Not an approval: no seat accepts its own work by handing it over.
