## Why one name
The address is internal. A person sees the title. The page holds the title. The face does not.
Face 04 carries the inherited page title. The inherited view is never stored.
The full name is the scan. Compute reads the rank, the version, and a 09 without opening the file.
An address without a title locates a slot. It does not say what is in it.

## Coordinate
The coordinate is the digit axes, and it ends at the first token that is not an axis.
DD.title is as valid as DD.SS.BB.PPPP.title. A shorter address is not a broken one.
Desk, shelf, and book are two digits. A page is four. A face is two, 01 through 08.
A face does not wear the page title. It is a child of the page. For now it is a .md file. Later it is a row.
Dots inside the coordinate are rank. Dots after the coordinate are not.
Left outranks right. A present axis governs downward. It does not bleed upward.
The coordinate is the internal key. The title is not the key. The coordinate is not permission.

## Hierarchy
Any axis, whatever its width, reserves 0 through 9. 01 and 0001 are the same rank. They are the highest points we recognize.
They are written 01 through 09 because an axis is two digits at minimum.
Hierarchy exists in the reserved band, unless a page states otherwise.
Outside that band, peers do not outrank. 20 does not parent 21 unless the proof says so. Proximity is not lineage.
Page reserved is 0000 through 0999. That is the same reservation, at page width.

## Envelope
The filename may carry more than the coordinate.
Order is coordinate, then the title, then version if it is a draft, then 09 if it is retired.
09 is only the end of the tail. Nothing follows it. A desk retires as DD.title.09.
Version is three digits, on a draft face. The seat has none.
Words carry no authority. Markers carry state, never rank.
A title starts with a letter, has no dots, and is unique under its parent.
