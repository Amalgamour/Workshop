## Scope
Name the scope first. The titles give the key words. A crawl to draft a new page checks whether the idea already exists. A draftroom, or another repo, is the place for that. The web is for a solution that already exists. Take the best, then invent the rest.

## Notes
A crawl notes as it gathers. A source with no note is lost. The note names the source and the sentence. It lands on the draft, or on face 06 of the page being learned. A crawl that does not note will bleed the intelligence.

## Choice
The choice is a later act. Crawl does not make it. A second crawl should add a source, not invent one.

## Not
Not walk. Walk checks that a page can be reached and does its job. Not align. Align is the faces of one page.
