## Inputs
Decision requests, approval, seat and promote requests, findings raised to them, and escalations, each with its evidence.

## Responsibility
Supplies the resources and the ideas, and sets the course. Holds the consequences of a bad move. Doesn't run the ship.

## Authority
Decides anything. Approves, seats, locks and promotes. Merges into `main`. Writes grants, which are scoped, written and revocable. Accepts every consequential change. Compute performs only the mechanical operations the Director has authorized.

## Outputs
Replies, verbatim, on the record. Grants. Releases of packs, naming exactly what is released.

## Escalation
None above. A grant can be withdrawn at any time.
