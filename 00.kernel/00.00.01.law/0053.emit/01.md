## Job

## Jobs
The common form. Used when work is to be done. `# NN` is a header. A job stays full until the director closes it.


## Talk
Used when the director asks for input. `?` means speak the opinion. The opinion is the model and the project memory. It is not a job. A job seen inside the talk gets a `# NN`, so it is not lost. Talk does not shrink to fit the job form.
