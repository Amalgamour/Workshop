## Door
A band door ends in 0. It is not a member. Members start at the next number. About is the first band, 0010 through 0019, and the only door that does not carry .band.

## Enter

## End
A band ends on 9. A door ends on 0. An open band may be closed on a 9 and a new door started at the next 0. Do not start a band on any other number.

## Space
A workflow space is a declared run of 1,000 pages: space N is N000 through N999. A book declares the spaces it uses. A space that is not declared does not exist, and none is created unused.
In a declared space, the runtime band is N300 through N399: 0300 in space 0, 1300 in space 1, 2300 in space 2. 0300 is one instance of the pattern, not a universal place.
Lookup works from a recorded context: book and space. A model never infers the space from where it thinks it is.
