## Two rhymes
Kernel books `01`–`09` match that face. Book `03` is where a model meets the clerk. Books `10` and up do not rhyme.
Inside a book, page `0001`–`0009` matches that face. Page `0003` is that book's clerk question, or it is absent.
Absence is allowed. A wrong body is not. `0006` is notes. `0007` is reserved. Do not fill it. Do not call it fixture or unset.

## The cross
The page is one axis. The face is the other.

Same integers. Opposite questions. At the book, check both directions. `0008` face `01` and `0001` face `08` can both be valid. Do not collapse them. Do not stop after the first.
