Context: Prima asked "What should a new book inherit from its shelf?" (proposed: how to work only; other books' decisions are reference).

> Book inherits from the shelf.  The shelf inherits from the desk.  The desk inherits from the kernel.

## What this sets
- The inheritance chain: kernel > desk > shelf > book. Each level inherits from the one above it.
- Still open: whether what passes down includes decisions (numbers, choices) or only how to work; and whether a book may override what it inherits.
