Question: [QD13](QUESTIONS-FOR-DIRECTOR.md) / kernel Q30 — What are the
four sections below page 0100?
Source: Tester, 2026-11-11 evening. Related: R17, R7, R10, R21, R25;
the QD02 working path; CONFLICTS D13; seqAddr.md:219-223.

---

## The idea, in one line

Four sections, each with its own laws. Matthew's "0000-0019 are
static" is a property of the first three sections together, not a
single section. That is how we get four sections and also his two-way
split (static vs working).

---

## The recommended four sections

1. `0000` — the door (the book's identity; only face 00, R10 and R25).
2. `0001-0009` — overrides (R7; none while the app is built, R21).
3. `0010-0019` — required static pages (contents, dictionary,
   concordance, and the common questions sample at 0013).
4. `0020-0099` — working pages, "not just locations." Inside this
   section, two kinds (not two extra sections): `0020-0049` behavior,
   `0050-0099` structure. R7 still uses those two ranges as override
   targets.

This is a missed option (Q30 option C / QD13 "another split"). It is
not QD13 option A (`0000-0009`, `0010-0019`, `0020-0049`,
`0050-0099`).

The rows above 0100 (`0100-0999` bands, `1000-9998` user space,
`9999` continuation) are not part of this cut. seqAddr.md:219-223 and
the 0051 Book Layout card today list five rows below 0100; on this
path those five become the four sections above.

---

## Why not the other options

Why not option A: it bundles the door and the overrides into one
section. Those have opposite laws — a door may hold only face 00; an
override has content faces and must state four things (R7). That
clashes with the QD02 working path (a type, and a section, only when
the laws differ) and with "each one has its own important."

Why not treat `0000-0019` as one section and then split working pages
into behavior and structure: that is only three sections, and Matthew
asked for four.

Why kinds inside section 4, not two more sections: "0020-0099 aren't
just locations" names one working range. Behavior vs structure is a
difference of role, the way `pointer` uses `kind`. If Matthew's "each
has its own important" was aimed at those two roles, option A is the
coherent alternative — its cost is still bundling the door with
overrides.

---

## What this does not change

It does not change R21 or R7's two target ranges. The 0051 Book
Layout card, CONFLICTS D13, and the seqAddr table have not been
rewritten. This unblocks those as a working path only.

For Matthew, one idea: the four sections are the door, the overrides,
the required static pages, and the working pages. The first three are
static. Working pages aren't just locations: some are behavior, some
are structure.
