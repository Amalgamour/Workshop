What the work order shows, in order:
1. Page words: the current face 01 and face 04. If face 04 is empty, say so. Do not copy them into the pack.
2. Frame: the packed jobs, from the pack text. If that text has a Builder line, show it. If not, say builder: not named.
3. Claim: who claimed, and the entry number. If the claim entry has a worker name (page 0414), show that too.
4. Return: the recorded text, its entry, its hash, the commit, and the file. The hash is of the trimmed text, not the raw file bytes (src/git.rs:11).
5. Check: verdict, checker, commit checked.
6. Signoff: outcome and note.
7. Close: the close record.
8. Notes: face 06 entries by entry number, from the note pinned at draft through this pass's close. Never because the text starts with "on 05.NN:".

Locks, said the way the code works:
