## The idea, in one line

Split the question. Do not seat notes (06). Do seat dictionary
sections, after a hostile trial, the way we seat lasting content
faces (01, 02, 04), not the way we climb and reseat a 05 pack.

This matches Designer's leftover sketch, with a sharper reason than
"two exceptions" to "a counted face is never seated."

---

## Why the old line is stale, and the cleaner law

The old line (seqAddr.md:199) is already stale: R9 removed the FF.00
board it depended on, and R22 already seats 05. The cleaner law is
that counters are used in two ways:

1. **Workflow counters** (05 tasks, 06 notes) climb with tails (draft,
   pack, open, return, review, or bare). Face 05 is seated as the best
   pack (R22-R24). A 06 note is a session record ("one notes per
   session," seqAddr.md:155). When the session is done, that note
   closes bare. It is evidence, not law, so it is not seated. If
   something in a note should last, it is merged into a content face
   and seated there.
2. **Section counters** (dictionary 03.14, 03.16, and so on) are
   lasting content, numbered into ranges. They may be seated after a
   hostile trial (R2, R20, axiom 9). They do not use workflow tails.
   This is the dictionary half of Q35 option B.

---

## Why not the other options

Why not Q35 option A for both (nothing on a counted face is seated):
that would fit notes, and would refuse a seat for word meanings that
should stand as lasting law. "Bare once reviewed" is not the same as
a trial and a seat.

Why not seat 06 the way we seat 05: a "best note" mixes two jobs.
Notes keep growing every session, like a chronicle. The 05 pack is a
best version of the work. Those are different shapes.

## What this does not decide

Q22 and QD11 (which faces are counted on which page types) stay open.
The builder already refuses any seated counter other than 05 until
this is ruled (`build.mjs:651`); on this path, that refusal would
lift for dictionary sections only, after a trial. That change has
not been made.

For Matthew, one idea: notes are a record of one session, so they
close when the session is done, and we do not seat them. Dictionary
sections are lasting word meanings, so they may be seated after a
trial, like other lasting pages.
