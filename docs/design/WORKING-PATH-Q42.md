Question: kernel Q42 — R22 asks for a face 05 on "each page", but R10
says a door page 0000 holds only face 00. Where do a door page's
lessons and pack go?
Source: Tester, 2026-11-11 evening. Related: R10, R22, R25; the QD02
and Q30 working paths; Q26, Q33.

---

## The idea, in one line

A door page gets no face 05. That honours R10 (page 0000 holds only
face 00) and R25 (face 00 is not seated the same way). This is not
quite option A, B, or C.

R22's "each page" means each page that may have content faces. A
door is excluded by R10. Its remaining work is already open questions
(Q33, Q26, Q31), and face 00 is already handled under R25, not by a
05 pack. Useful lessons in the four parked packs should be folded
into 0051 and 0052's own packs or notes. The parked files stay as
history and are not built.

---

## Why not A, B, or C as written

Why not B (let 0000 hold face 05 as an exception to R10): R10 is the
more specific rule, and the QD02 working path already gives door
pages their own type with that law. An exception would put content
faces back on the page that is only a registry.

Why not A as written (put the pack on the container's first working
page, for example 0010): 0010 is a required static page (contents, in
the Q30 working path), not a dumping ground. The parked door packs
are almost the same on every door — rule the registry fields, decide
state and back-links, trial face 00. That is door-type work, already
explained on kernel pages 0051 (sentinels and the door rule) and
0052 (face 00). The parked packs even link there. Putting those
steps on 0010 would mix two pages' work.

Why not C (the pack lives in the parent container's pack): that mixes
levels (a shelf door's work would sit on the desk). The kernel door
has no clean parent.

---

## What this does not decide

Q26 (where a door keeps a back-link) and Q33 (door registry fields)
stay open.

For Matthew, one idea: a door page has only face 00, so it has no
work pack on 05. What we learned about doors already lives on the
pages that explain doors. The parked packs stay as history, and are
not built.
