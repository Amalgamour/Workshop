Question: [QD10](QUESTIONS-FOR-DIRECTOR.md) — Is the dictionary's
03.00 a door?
Source: Tester, 2026-11-11 evening. Related: R16, R9, R1, Q12; the
QD03 working path; kernel Q29; CONFLICTS K03 and D12; face00 F4.

---

## The idea, in one line

There is no `03.00` file. The dictionary still has a way in — a menu
of its sections — but that menu is worked out from the sections that
exist (`03.10` and so on), not written as a face-00 counter. This is
QD10 option B, plus the same shape as the QD03 working path (lists
are derived, not stored as a special 00).

---

## Why this honours both of Matthew's words

The clash may be a word clash, not a design clash. Q12 asked "menu or
door?" Option B in that question was "the door of a counted face,
listing counters." He said "door" at 3:11 PM, eight minutes after R9
("faces do not have sentinels; counted faces have no door"). In R1,
"door" is the all-zero sentinel on a desk, shelf, book or page — and
R9 took that word off faces. Tester does not read R16 as undoing R9
for one page. The reading is: the dictionary has an entrance, a place
you look to see the sections. That job can exist as a derived view,
without a `03.00` file. Faces still have no sentinels.

---

## Why not the other options

Why not QD10 option A (the dictionary is the one exception, and
`03.00` is a real door): an exception to R9 would bring face sentinels
back, and would push the same exception for `03.99` (continuation),
which R9 also forbids and which the kernel already struck. The
builder already refuses a written counter `00`, with a held note
about R16 (`build.mjs:157`). Nothing is built on `03.00` today (no
such file under `kernel/`), so we are not tearing down a working
piece.

Why not a written menu card on face 03 instead of a derived list: a
card is fine as a person-facing explanation, but the list of sections
should be derived, like boards (format.md:102) and like the face list
(QD03). A hand-written menu would go stale when a section is added.
