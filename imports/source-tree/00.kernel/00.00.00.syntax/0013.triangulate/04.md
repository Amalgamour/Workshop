## Triangulate
Why: because one face is a claim, not a solve.

Body: Open the named pieces and read them against each other. Two that agree are a reading. A disagree is a hole. A missing face stays missing. The scan is law page `0018.seqaddr`. ## Address
Why: because an address with no title cannot be looked up.

Body: The full address is `DD.SS.BB.PPPP.Title`. `DD.title` is a desk title. `DD.SS.title` is a shelf title. `DD.SS.BB.title` is a book title. `DD.SS.BB.PPPP.title` is a page title. Shelf number `00` does not exist. There is no shelf sentinel. `DD.00.BB` is the address of the desk books. Law and syntax live there, including a local override. It is not a shelf. Each rung needs its title. An address with no title cannot triangulate, however many axes it has. A shorter address is still an address.

## Navigation
Why: because a short cite with no book cannot be found.

Body: Four digits are the homing mark. Inside a page, two digits are a face. Inside the book, `PPPP.title.FF`. A book, a shelf, and a desk take a tail only to explain a change. `00.kernel.draft` names a desk. `draft` is the working copy. No tail is the live file.

## Construction

## Face
Why: because a one-digit mark will be obeyed as a rank.

Body: A one-digit mark is an axiom. It is not an axis. An axis is two digits at minimum. A face is 01 through 08. 09 is the end of the tail, not a face.
