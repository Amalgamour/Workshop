## Job

source: [01 / Job](01.md#job); [02 / Job](02.md#job)

why: Routing should reduce reading without replacing the source.

Face 03 is a header and a menu of named sections. A short explanation is optional. Each entry says what the section provides and links to the exact available face-section targets. Selecting a menu entry does not execute a command or establish a permission.

## Selection

source: [01 / Selection](01.md#selection); [02 / Selection](02.md#selection)

why: The menu is the point at which narrow retrieval becomes possible.

The model selects the needed representations of a section. A section may offer 01, 02, 04, and a relevant 08 relationship. It does not require all of them to be opened together. A rule question needs its governing 01; a compact use may need 02 plus the governing source when conditions are unclear.

## Integrity

source: [01 / Integrity](01.md#integrity); [02 / Integrity](02.md#integrity)

why: A menu can look healthy while sending the reader to a reassigned page.

Advertise only supplied targets. An absent optional face is omitted. A required missing target is a finding. A source link outside this page is explicitly identified as external. Rebuild the menu after section changes and verify each route; do not preserve an old route merely because its page number still exists.

## Omit

Why: The job of the menu. A selection does not permit.

The menu lists a section that is on the page. A face that is absent has no line. Selecting a line does not run a command and does not permit.
