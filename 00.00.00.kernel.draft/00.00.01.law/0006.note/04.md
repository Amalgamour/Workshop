## Placement

source: [01 / Placement](01.md#placement); [02 / Placement](02.md#placement)

why: Placement makes the next sitting able to find the reason where the work lives.

An insight lives with the page or active draft it concerns. A separate working note may capture the sitting before its findings are placed in the return. Face 06 is page-local memory, not a dump of unrelated ideas.

The compact form selects the useful handle: Subject page/draft -> note. Sitting capture -> working notes -> placed findings.

## Lineage

source: [01 / Lineage](01.md#lineage); [02 / Lineage](02.md#lineage)

why: The record carries continuity that model memory cannot supply.

Record what was observed, its source, what changed, why, and what remains unresolved. Do not rewrite past notes into a false history. Marks on an active draft are absorbed visibly into its next version rather than forgotten.

The compact form selects the useful handle: Observation; source; proposed/change state; why; unresolved.

## Boundary

source: [01 / Boundary](01.md#boundary); [02 / Boundary](02.md#boundary)

why: Unprompted insight is valuable precisely when its state and destination remain visible.

A note can reveal a missing rule or trigger a proposed task, but does not itself change law or mint a ticket. Overflow belongs with its subject; an unrelated thought is not made binding because it appeared during a lawful write.

The compact form selects the useful handle: Insight -> record -> classify -> authorized next work. No silent law change. Overflow stays with its subject.
