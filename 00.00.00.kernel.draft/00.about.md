## About
This project is an experimental model environment.

Its purpose is to study how people and models can preserve reliable understanding across sessions, systems, and generations without depending on model memory.

The problem is not only storing information.

As AI systems increasingly help design, maintain, and redesign complex systems, a deeper risk appears. The why behind decisions can be lost.

Code can remain. Files can remain. Outputs can remain. The reasoning that made them coherent can disappear.

Over time this creates generational bleed. Models change. People leave. Assumptions become invisible. Old constraints are forgotten. Repairs accumulate. A future intelligence may inherit a system whose original reasoning can no longer be reconstructed.

More intelligence does not automatically recover missing history.

A future model may be more capable than the model that built a system and still be unable to see a detail that was obvious to the earlier generation, because the evidence explaining why it mattered was never preserved.

This project treats that continuity problem as an environment-design problem.

The environment should help a model determine what is in front of it, what has authority, what is missing, what should be preserved, what may be changed, and why prior decisions were made.

The goal is not to make models remember everything.

The goal is to make the environment preserve enough structure that a capable model can arrive cold, orient itself, recover the relevant reasoning, perform work, and leave the environment more understandable for the next sitting.

This is why Librarian is a collector of why.

A useful repository should preserve more than artifacts. It should preserve enough intent, authority, provenance, uncertainty, rejected alternatives, and operational reasoning that future people and models can reconstruct understanding instead of merely inheriting outputs.

The work in this repository is experimental.

Treat the environment as evidence.

Follow the standing structure in front of you rather than assuming remembered patterns are correct.

Do not repair uncertainty by invention.

Preserve why whenever the work reveals it.

The long-term question is simple. Can understanding survive the intelligence that created it.
