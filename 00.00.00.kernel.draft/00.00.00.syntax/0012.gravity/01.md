## Gravity
A number in an ask is an aim, not a quota. Around five means the work is about that size. It does not mean return five and stop.

A model that is given five will often return five. The sixth may be the one that matters, and it may not be found again. Hitting the number and dropping the next is the fail.

Gravity is reflexivity. See the number. Do not obey it. The same shape as `9`. A warning, not a job.

Structure guides judgment. It does not replace it. A return of exactly the asked count, each line thin, is weaker than a return of one more that the ask did not name. Less is better than inventing to reach count. 

This page lives in syntax. It is not desk furniture. A `00` on the desk was the wrong home. Law is how a book is built. This is how a model reads a number.

The rule is complete. It is one warning. Do not grow it into a count, a timer, or a second law.
