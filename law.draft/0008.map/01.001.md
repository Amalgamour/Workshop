## Job

owner: kernel-only

Face 08 records where a design or relationship is found, with source custody where needed. It does not govern the target. A section route on face 03, a contents listing, and a parent proof are different records, even when they mention the same page.

## Relationships

owner: kernel-only

This sample proposes typed relationship rows: member-of for idea grouping, continues-from for an immediate work chain, and references for an ordinary dependency or useful pointer. These labels describe connections and grant no authority. A structural parent still requires its existing lawful proof. No global single-parent rule is inferred for ideas.

## Resolution

owner: kernel-only

Resolve an address with the existing syntax walk and seqaddr grammar. Then inspect the named relationship or subject section. Do not walk every reference as a parent, scan sideways to guess a missing source, or choose by numerical proximity. An explicit source link may be followed for the named retrieval job; that is not an authority climb.

## Overrides

owner: kernel-only

An exception row needs the exact source section, proposed target scope, difference, reason, and the approving record if one exists. A map row never approves itself. Existing law 0001 assigns an override role to its 08, while law 0008 places similar language on its 01. This sample retains that as a recorded conflict requiring director resolution; it does not create a functioning override registry.
