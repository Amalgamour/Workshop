## Authority

owner: kernel-only

Face 01 holds the normative conditions of its page. It governs those conditions only within the authority and subject scope that page lawfully owns. A filename, a draft stamp, a citation, or a section label grants no authority. This sample proposes a contract; it is not standing kernel law.

## Use

owner: kernel-only

A reader identifies the needed subject section, resolves its governing source, and opens the available representation needed for the job. Face 03 selects a route; face 01 establishes the rule; face 02 supplies its compact form; face 04 explains it. If a permission or condition is uncertain, reopen the governing file. A menu is not evidence that the rule has been read.

## Stamp

owner: kernel-only

A face begins with its face header and a stamp identifying version, authorization/state, face, page, book, keyword, why, and any relevant intent cite. Values must describe the actual artifact. This proposed complete stamp is not copied blindly from a live face. Draft names carry a three-digit version; seats do not. Exact lifecycle ownership stays with the existing version/approval laws.

## Sections

owner: kernel-only

Each section has a unique heading within its face and a declared job. The heading is the section key within the page, not a new seqaddr axis. In this sample, matching sections keep the exact same heading across faces 01, 02, and 04. A rename is a recorded change to the section and its routes together. Subject sections may be added; the contract does not prescribe one universal list of headings for every page.

## Construction

owner: kernel-only

Write normative conditions on face 01, compact forms on face 02, routes on face 03, and recoverable explanation on face 04. A normative section states its scope, applicable conditions, required or forbidden result, and how a missing or conflicting source is reported. Those elements may be prose; they need not become repetitive field labels. Keep shell commands, routing tables, and extended demonstrations in their owning representations. References establishing a rule source are allowed: they are not executable commands.

## Admission

owner: kernel-only

A condition belongs in face 01 only if this page owns that subject and the statement changes what is required, allowed, or forbidden. A why, an observation, a proposed change, an unassigned idea, a task, and a navigation hint are not silently admitted as law. When authority is unresolved, retain the proposal and report the uncertainty; do not infer an override.

## Scope

owner: kernel-only

Each proposed section declares one owner class: kernel-only, desk-local, or book-local. These names are design metadata, not new numeric ranks. Kernel-only face mechanics are inherited. Desk-local policy applies within its lawful desk boundary. Book-local subject conditions apply only to their book. A local page 0001 may supply subject law without supplying or replacing the face contract. An approved exception must identify the exact source section and target scope; metadata alone cannot approve it.

## Inheritance

owner: kernel-only

For each requested subject section, use the existing lawful container walk and declared parent proof. A local definition is usable only if it owns that subject and scope and agrees with inherited constraints, or has a lawful named approved exception. A local pointer continues lookup at its named source. An absent optional representation does not start a search. A required unresolved governing section is a defect. Detect loops and conflicting claims, keep the conflicting artifacts, and report the exact section rather than guessing from proximity.

## Correspondence

owner: kernel-only

A supplied face 02 or 04 identifies the face 01 section it represents. Its conditions must agree with that section, while its form may differ. In this sample face 04 covers all supplied rule/card sections; face 02 is selective where a useful compact form exists. Face 03 inventories the supplied section routes. Face 08 appears only for actual relationships or source custody; it does not have to repeat every content section. A disagreement is recorded for authority review, not settled by majority vote.

## Validation

owner: kernel-only

Before returning a face draft, check its identity, state, unique headings, section ownership, actual route targets, source pointers, and agreement across supplied representations. Check required coverage separately from optional absence. A valid shape is not approval. Automated checks must report structural failures and unresolved meaning separately; neither a successful parse nor a model self-check clears a lock.
