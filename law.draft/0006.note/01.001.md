## Placement

owner: kernel-only

An insight lives with the page or active draft it concerns. A separate working note may capture the sitting before its findings are placed in the return. Face 06 is page-local memory, not a dump of unrelated ideas.

## Lineage

owner: kernel-only

Record what was observed, its source, what changed, why, and what remains unresolved. Do not rewrite past notes into a false history. Marks on an active draft are absorbed visibly into its next version rather than forgotten.

## Boundary

owner: kernel-only

A note can reveal a missing rule or trigger a proposed task, but does not itself change law or mint a ticket. Overflow belongs with its subject; an unrelated thought is not made binding because it appeared during a lawful write.
