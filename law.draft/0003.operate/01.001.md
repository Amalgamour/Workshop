## Job

owner: kernel-only

Face 03 is a header and a menu of named sections. A short explanation is optional. Each entry says what the section provides and links to the exact available face-section targets. Selecting a menu entry does not execute a command or establish a permission.

## Selection

owner: kernel-only

A clerk or model selects the needed representations of a section. A section may offer 01, 02, 04, and a relevant 08 relationship. It does not require all of them to be opened together. A rule question needs its governing 01; a compact use may need 02 plus the governing source when conditions are unclear.

## Integrity

owner: kernel-only

Advertise only supplied targets. An absent optional face is omitted. A required missing target is a finding. A source link outside this page is explicitly identified as external. Rebuild the menu after section changes and verify each route; do not preserve an old route merely because its page number still exists.
