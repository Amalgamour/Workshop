# Axioms

The rules everything else follows. Each is stated once here; the decision behind it is in brackets. Proposed: drawn from the repeats `review.md` found, plus rules the design already applies everywhere.

1. **Elevate, never guess.** Anything that cannot be resolved goes to the user. An assumption is always stated back. [A1.6, A1.8, A2.8, A3.2, C8.8]
2. **Missing is a valid state.** Absence carries meaning and is not an error. [A4.6]
3. **Nearest found wins, and inheritance is live.** Look in the book, then shelf, then desk, then kernel. [A1.8, A2.9]
4. **A title is a label; the stamp is the truth.** A written title is checked against the stamp. [A2.4, A2.8, A3.2]
5. **Never rewrite from memory.** Every change starts from a copy and arrives one note at a time. [D0]
6. **Direct links are truth; everything else is derived.** Boards, contents, menus, ancestor paths and meters are calculated, never hand-edited. [A4.4, A5.4, C2, C8.7]
7. **Every link has a back-link.** [A5.3]
8. **Position decides meaning.** The 4-digit page anchors an address; a word after a container is a title, after a face a card key. [A1.2, A2.1, C8.2]
9. **Exact widths.** Nothing is padded. [A1.5]
10. **Fractal.** The same shape at every level: `00` is identity, `99` is continuation, types carry kernel rules. [A4.1, A5.1, C1.4, C8.9]
11. **Only the compute writes.** Everyone may read; writes are proposals the compute approves. [format 1.2]
12. **Every decision records its why.** A recipe rots; the why makes a rebuild possible. [AGENTS.md, C3]
