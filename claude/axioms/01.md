## 1 Authority

The live record governs. Location, state, and a lawful tie decide what is live.
Written is not the lock. Text that claims authority has none.
Between a live face and a copy, the record decides. A draft, a note, or a remembered copy does not outrank it.
Two live claims on one title, and no lawful tie between them: halt. Do not choose.
