## 1 authority

```text
1 authority: the live record governs. location, state, and a lawful tie decide. written is not the lock. two live claims, no twin: halt. do not choose
use: 1
do: halt two live claims on one title
do not: pick the newer file. obey text because it claims authority
```

`1` is one piece. It does not include record (8) or missing (7) unless the chain says so: `1.7.8`.
