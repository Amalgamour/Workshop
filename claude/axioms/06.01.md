## Axiom 1: questions for the Director

1. **Twin: removed (Director).** Twin was there for recovery and to say which side governs when two things coexist. Both jobs now have stronger forms: the log keeps full history, drafting copies from the file, every 01 card has its 04 card, bands link sets by number, and a named override ties a child to its parent. The halt now reads "no lawful tie between them", so axiom 1 uses its own term instead of a separate concept.
2. **Axiom 8 becomes connections (Director direction).** Proposed:
   - Record's content is already carried by axiom 1 ("the live record governs", "a remembered copy does not outrank it"), so memory stays in 1.
   - 8 holds connections across desks, books, pages and cards: a pointer to a connection. `7.8` is a missing connection.
   - Name: **Connection**, and axiom 1 says "lawful connection" instead of "lawful tie", so both use one word.
   - Cold set `1.7.8` becomes authority, missing, connection: before a write, check what governs, what is missing, and what it is connected to.
3. **Who resolves a halt:** open. Explanation given to the Director; awaiting a choice between naming the Director in axiom 1 or citing `1.3`.

## Cite (Director direction)

A cite conveys a large missing in a very small package. Any card that needs to change can carry one: `cite: 7.8` says a connection is missing. Proposed: `cite` is the field name (the kernel's operate page already uses `cite: 3.5`), optional on every card and on the page stamp.
