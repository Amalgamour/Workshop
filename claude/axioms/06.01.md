## Axiom 1: settled with the Director

Final form:

> Authority is granted, never claimed. A person holds it, in full or in part, and grants it on. Compute enforces it. A model works within it. That is the order. The live record is where authority is kept: location, state, and a lawful connection decide what is live. A draft, a copy, or a memory does not outrank it. Two live claims with no lawful connection: halt, and raise it to whoever holds more. Do not choose.

What changed from the original, and why:

- **Granted, never claimed** replaces "Written is not the lock". Same rule, stated as what does decide.
- **The order** (person, compute, model) is new. Axiom 1 now gives each of the three its agency and says whose wins. The Director found it more complete than the old axiom.
- **Levels** (developer `00`, lower developer `01`, magic user `04`) are explained on face 04, not in the axiom, so the axiom stays pure.
- **The halt rises up the order.** This answers who resolves a halt without naming a role. A magic user can still resolve one; compute and the model do most of the work.
- **Twin removed.** "No twin" became "no lawful connection". The log, drafting, 01/04 cards, bands and named overrides now do twin's two jobs (recovery, and which side governs).
- **Record's content** ("the file governs", memory does not) is carried by "the live record" and "a draft, a copy, or a memory does not outrank it", freeing axiom 8.

Open from axiom 1:

- Levels `02` and `03` (Director): additional states for someone with desk or shelf authority, which a model or a user can hold. What each grants exactly is still to define. Question: are they face reach (like 00, 01, 04) or scope markers? Scope already comes from where a grant is attached.
- Scope (Director, settled): authority depends on the environment. Desk > shelf > book. A grant reaches down from where it is attached, never up or sideways.

## Axiom 2: settled with the Director

Final form:

> The same shape at every scale. Inherit what is agreed; write only the difference. Do not copy the parent, and do not reinvent what already exists: both create drift. A child refines its parent; it does not replace it. A difference that contradicts the parent needs a named override, approved by the authority above it.

How it got here:

Fractal now speaks only about the shape staying consistent.
- "Do not reinvent what already exists" was added: inventing what exists creates drift, as copying does.
- "The book is the rebuild" moved to face 04, with its reason: the book holds the whys.
- The list of lawful rebuild sources (log, other faces, back-links) was taken out for now.

Moved out of 2, and where to:

Override approval (Director): approved by the authority above the child, per the user levels (`00` admin, kernel only; `01` power user, up to desk; `04` basic user; `05-09` specialist; `02`, `03` to define).

Missing (Director): explicitly marking something not complete narrows and focuses a model, instead of it inventing a solution to the hole. Carry this into axiom 7 as its why.

## Axiom 3: settled with the Director

Final form:

> Person, model, and compute are three distinct forms of intelligence. Each leads the part of the work it does best. The work needs all three, and each is given the work in its own form. Name the lead with M (model), P (person), and C (compute), in order: `3MPC` means the model leads, then the person, then compute. One or two letters may be named. A lead is not authority.

Named **Leads** (Director), replacing Dance. How it got here:

- Dance mixed two things: who does which job, and three kinds of intelligence. Axiom 1 now holds authority, its order and the division of work, so 3 keeps only the second. That is why it felt subservient to 1.
- Person, model and compute are three distinct forms of intelligence, like rock, paper, scissors: each does one piece better than another and worse than a third. This is the reason for faces: 01 for the model, 02-03 for compute, 04 for the person.
- A person can do everything the other two can, far slower and at great cost in life force. A lead names fit for the cost, not ability.
- Axiom 3 is the only cite with a modifier: M, P, C in order (`3MPC`). One or two letters may be named (`3C`).
- A hard solved why should be coded for compute; then it is a question a model can ask and an action a person can take.
- The software-project table on face 04 is an illustration of the split, not a fixed list.

Moved out of 3:

- "A menu points. It does not become law": axiom 8 (Connection). Cite `3.8`.
- "Sync does not grant permission": covered by axiom 1, "granted, never claimed".
- "Clerk": an implementation detail, dropped.
- "Talk is not work": proposed for axiom 6 (Lineage), since only a visible, recorded change counts. Not yet decided.

Grammar to record: a chain may carry `3` followed by one to three distinct letters from M, P, C, with no dot. Add to the chain rules in `seqAddr.md` when the grammar is next updated.

## Axiom 4: settled with the Director

Final form:

> A law without its why is incomplete: its reasoning cannot be understood. Record the why when the choice is made. When a why is lost, recover it in proportion to the cost of being wrong. Never invent a reason so the work feels finished.

How it got here:

- Why is the heart of the work. A law without a why is flawed, because its reasoning cannot be fully understood (Director). That sentence now opens the axiom.
- "Record the why when the choice is made" was added. The original only covered recovering a lost why; AGENTS.md is about recording one.
- "Unknown stays unknown" moved to axiom 7. Cite `4.7` for a missing why.
- Whys live on face 04 at every scale: card, page, book (page 0000's face 04). No `why:` on stamps.
- A face 01 card is the packed form of its 04 card: minimum words for a model. When it is not enough, the model requests the why from compute, which finds it by the card key on face 04, like retrieval (Director).

When a 01 card and its 04 card disagree, neither governs: it is elevated to the user for correction (Director). This is axiom 1 applied: two live claims with no lawful connection halt and rise up the order.

Carry to axiom 7: "unknown stays unknown" (from 4), and the Director's why for missing: marking something not complete narrows and focuses a model instead of it inventing a solution.

## Axiom 5: settled with the Director

Final form:

> A model without a framed task sees far more paths than a person's mind can hold. Know what the work aims at, and why, before doing it: without the why, the cost of solving rises. Frame the work to the spirit of the goal, not only its words: what it is for, where, what to do, where to stop, and what done looks like. Within the frame, use full intelligence and think freely. An observation outside the frame is permission to report, not to act: return it, and raise it to authority. At the edge of the frame, stop, state the issue, record it, and raise it. Question the frame itself: is it the right size, and does it let the work finish?

Named **Frame** (Director), replacing Narrow. How it got here:

- Narrow described the size of the result. The axiom is about reducing the infinite between 0 and 1 to what the person means: a model trained on vast data sees far more paths than a person holds in mind, and struggles to meet a person's narrower view of reality.
- Face 05 (tasks) is the expression of it: a framed scope of work. Models are asked why the scope is what it is, whether it should be wider or narrower, whether it lets the work finish, and whether it meets the spirit of the goal, not only the prompt (a house, not a birdhouse).
- Maximum freedom to think, with the intelligence focused on one point.
- Useful observation is permission to report, not to act. Observations are elevated to authority, never assumed (Director).
- "Hold" meant: stop, state the issue, record it, then elevate to authority (Director).
- The soldering-bench image was an example to understand the idea, not wording for the axiom; the why line says it plainly instead.
- Source material: the pack-lint band set (`10.10.12.rebuild`, `0417`, `0517`, `0117`), now the worked example on face 04.

Carry to section D (task cards):

- Real pack shape: read first, do, stop if, file; items with a verb tag (`>build`, `>note`, `>review`), reason, recommend, path, scale, ask, status; "before you finish" questions.
- Real return shape: did, blocked, need; item statuses; unprompted; overflow.
- Decisions carry issue, options, and what each does; a stop list names what reaches the Director.
- Where returns live: the workflow puts a return on the 01NN returns page (`0117/05.01.return.md`); the tail design keeps it on the counter (`05.01.return`). One must change.
- Closed returns are not appended to ("Do not hand more lines onto ..."). Relevant to axiom 6.

Carry to bands (A2.6): the workflow's real band meanings are 1 returns, 2 code, 3 compute, 4 design, 5 packs. The A2.6 example (4 dev, 5 build) should match.
