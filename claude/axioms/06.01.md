## Axiom 1: questions for the Director

1. **Shared titles.** The axiom halts on "two live claims on one title". The new design lets several pages share a title on purpose (A2.4): band sets such as `0423.timer` and `0523.timer`. Why does the halt hold? Because nothing says how two claims relate. A band set does say: same `N` and `NN`, different band. **Is a band set a twin?** If yes, the axiom stands as written and band sets are its lawful exception.
2. **Memory in 1 or 8.** Axiom 1 says a "remembered copy" does not outrank the record. Axiom 8 says "memory may hold the form and still not govern." Why say it twice? In 1 it is about rank, in 8 about the file before a write. **Keep "remembered copy" in 1, or leave memory to 8** and cite `1.8` when both apply?
3. **Who resolves a halt.** "Halt. Do not choose." says to stop, not who decides. Why leave it out? Because who decides is a role, and roles are axiom 3. **Keep 1 pure and let `1.3` carry the handoff**, or name the Director in 1?
