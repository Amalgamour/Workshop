# Launch checklist (v3)
Replaces the v2 checklist. Five steps.

1. Sign out the boat in the log.
2. Check the river level board. Do not launch above amber.
   Reason: added after the October 2024 capsize, when a crew launched on red.
3. Check the hull and the riggers. If launching before sunrise, check the bow light.
4. Everyone wears a life jacket. Compulsory for all rowers under 18, and for everyone from 1 November to 31 March (club rules, rule 5).
5. Everyone knows the capsize drill (safety/capsize-drill.md). Carry the boat with the bow pointing upstream. Launch from the slipway, one crew at a time.
