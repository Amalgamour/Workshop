# Launch checklist (v3)
Replaces v2. Five steps.
1. Sign out the boat in the log.
2. Check the river level board. Do not launch above amber. (Reason: added after the October 2024 capsize, when a crew launched on red.)
3. Check the boat and kit: hull and riggers; the bow light if launching before sunrise; life jackets on, where rule 5 or 6 of rules/club.md applies.
4. Everyone knows the capsize drill (safety/capsize-drill.md).
5. Carry the boat bow pointing upstream and launch from the slipway, one crew at a time.

How v2 became v3 (so the decision can be rebuilt):
- v2 steps 3 and 4 (hull/riggers, bow light) merged into one kit check, with life jackets added because the new rules require them.
- v2 steps 6 and 7 (carrying, launching) merged into one step.
- Nothing was dropped. Step 2 keeps its reason, because it is the only step whose reason is recorded and it is the one most likely to be cut by someone who does not know the history.
- The other v2 steps have no recorded reason. I did not invent any.
