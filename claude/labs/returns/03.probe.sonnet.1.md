# Probe 03: reading chains

1. `7.8` - A connection is missing (a link that cannot be opened). Do not repair it from memory or from a similar-looking piece; report it and raise it to whoever holds authority.

2. `8.7` - A connection exists but is incomplete (partly written, e.g. no back-link or only partly stated). Use what is written, do not fill the rest by guessing, and report the incompleteness.

3. `4.7` - The why is missing. Do not invent a reason; recover it in proportion to the cost of being wrong, or leave it missing and report it if within the frame.

4. `7.4` - Something missing that concerns why, read in the order missing then why: the hole is a gap whose reasoning is the issue, i.e. ask why it is missing. Reader should ask why it is absent before trying to fill it, and not invent it. (Order differs from `4.7`: here the missing-ness leads and the why refines it.)

5. `6.8` - Lineage, refined by connection: the line of descent is traveled by written connections. Follow the written links back to what governs this piece and forward to what depends on it; do not rely on matching or similar pieces.

6. `8.6` - A connection, refined by lineage: the connection to follow is the lineage, the written link to its ancestors and dependents. Check where the link sits in the line and raise it if the trail is stale.

7. `9.5` - Not yet tried (trial pending), refined by frame: do not trust this as it stands. It needs a hostile review, and that review should be framed (what it aims at, where to stop) and should stay inside the frame, reporting anything outside it.

8. `2.6` - Fractal, refined by lineage: this piece inherits from its parent. Read the parent through the line, write only the difference, and any contradiction needs a named override linked back to who authorized it.

9. `1.7.9` - Authority, missing, then trial: before acting, check the authority; something is missing and without written permission it stays missing; and the result has not faced its trial. Reader should not fill the hole, should not trust the piece, and should report and raise it.

10. `3C` - Compute leads this part of the work. The reader should give the work to compute in its own form (checks, enforcement, mechanical work); this is a lead, not authority.
