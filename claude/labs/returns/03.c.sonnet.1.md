# Launch checklist (v3)
Status: draft. 9: not yet through its trial, not approved. Replaces procedures/launch-v2.md only once approved. Until then v2 stays the live checklist.

1. Sign out the boat in the log.
2. Check the river level board. Do not launch above amber. (Why: added after the October 2024 capsize, when a crew launched on red.)
3. Check the hull and the riggers, and the bow light if launching before sunrise.
4. Everyone knows the capsize drill. 7.8: the pointer safety/capsize-drill.md cannot be opened, so it is not linked here.
5. Carry the boat with the bow pointing upstream, and launch from the slipway, one crew at a time.

Why v3 is shaped this way: the committee found v2 too long (7 steps) and asked for 5 or fewer. I merged related steps and dropped none of v2's checks. The river-level reason is kept with step 2.
