# Launch checklist (v3)
1. Sign out the boat in the log.
2. Check the river level board. Do not launch above amber. Check the hull and the riggers.
3. Put on a life jacket. Compulsory for everyone from 1 November to 31 March, and for all rowers under 18.
4. Check everyone knows the capsize drill (safety/capsize-drill.md). If launching before sunrise, check the bow light.
5. Carry the boat with the bow pointing upstream. Launch from the slipway, one crew at a time.
