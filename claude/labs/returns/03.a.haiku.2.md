# Launch checklist (v3)
1. Sign out the boat in the log. Check the river level board: do not launch above amber. (Added after the October 2024 capsize, when a crew launched on red.)
2. Check the hull and the riggers. Check the bow light if launching before sunrise.
3. Check life jackets: compulsory for everyone 1 November to 31 March, and for all rowers under 18.
4. Everyone knows the capsize drill (safety/capsize-drill.md).
5. Carry the boat with the bow pointing upstream and launch from the slipway, one crew at a time.
