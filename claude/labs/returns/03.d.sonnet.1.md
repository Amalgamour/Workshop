# Launch checklist (v3)
Status: DRAFT of 15 November 2026. Not approved. Not yet reviewed by anyone other than its editor.
Descends from: procedures/launch-v2.md (kept as it was, not rewritten). The committee asked for a simpler v3 on 12 November 2026 (notes/2026-11-committee.md).
Method: no v2 check was dropped. The 7 steps are merged into 5.

1. Sign out the boat in the log.
2. Check the river level board. Do not launch above amber. Reason: added after the October 2024 capsize, when a crew launched on red.
3. Check the hull and the riggers. If launching before sunrise, check the bow light too.
4. Everyone knows the capsize drill (safety/capsize-drill.md). Link problem (7.8): this file does not exist, see rules/club.md rule 4.
5. Carry the boat with the bow pointing upstream and launch from the slipway, one crew at a time.
