# Harbor Rowing Club: club rules
These rules apply at both boathouses. A boathouse page lists only its differences.

## 1 Opening
From 1 November 2026 the boathouse opens at 7am (it previously opened at 6am) and closes at dusk.
Reason: not recorded in the committee notes. Needs the committee's reason before print.

## 2 Rowing solo
A member may row solo if cleared: safety test passed within the last 12 months, aged 18 or over, and swim test passed.
Reason: a solo rower has no one to help them in the water.

## 3 Guests
Members may bring a guest twice a season.
Reason: not recorded. Needs the committee's reason before print.

## 4 Safety
Every crew must know the capsize drill before going out (see safety/capsize-drill.md).
Reason: not recorded. Needs the committee's reason before print.

## 5 Life jackets: under 18
Life jackets are compulsory for all rowers under 18.
Reason: not recorded in the committee notes. Needs the committee's reason before print.

## 6 Life jackets: cold season
Life jackets are compulsory for everyone from 1 November to 31 March.
Reason: cold water shock can stop a strong swimmer swimming within a minute.

# Launch checklist (v3)
1. Sign out the boat in the log.
2. Check the river level board. Do not launch above amber. (Reason: added after the October 2024 capsize, when a crew launched on red.)
3. Check the hull and the riggers, and the bow light if launching before sunrise.
4. Everyone knows the capsize drill (safety/capsize-drill.md) and has a life jacket on where required (under 18, or 1 November to 31 March).
5. Carry the boat with the bow pointing upstream, and launch from the slipway, one crew at a time.

(5 steps. Nothing from v2 was dropped, only merged: v2 steps 3 and 4 became step 3, and v2 steps 6 and 7 became step 5. The life jacket check in step 4 is new, from the November rules. Keep launch-v2.md archived rather than deleted, because it holds the October 2024 reason.)
