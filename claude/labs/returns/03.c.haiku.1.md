=== procedures/launch-v3.md (new) ===

# Launch checklist (v3)
Supersedes procedures/launch-v2.md, which is kept as the record.

1. Sign out the boat in the log.
2. Check the river level board. Do not launch above amber. (Added after the October 2024 capsize, when a crew launched on red.)
3. Check the hull, the riggers, and the bow light if launching before sunrise.
4. Everyone knows the capsize drill (safety/capsize-drill.md) and is wearing a life jacket. Life jackets are compulsory for all under 18, and for everyone from 1 November to 31 March.
5. Carry the boat with the bow pointing upstream, and launch from the slipway, one crew at a time.

Changes from v2: v2 steps 3 and 4 are now step 3; v2 steps 6 and 7 are now step 5; the life jacket check is added in step 4 from the committee notes of 12 November 2026. Every v2 safety check is kept: the river level rule (step 2), the hull and riggers, and the bow light before sunrise (step 3). Seven steps become five.
