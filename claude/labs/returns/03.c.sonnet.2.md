# Launch checklist (v3) - DRAFT, not approved (9: has not faced its trial)
Replaces v2 once approved. Predecessor: procedures/launch-v2.md (7 steps).
1. Sign out the boat in the log.
2. Check the river level board. Do not launch above amber.
3. Check the hull and the riggers, and the bow light if launching before sunrise.
4. Everyone knows the capsize drill (safety/capsize-drill.md).
5. Carry the boat with the bow pointing upstream and launch from the slipway, one crew at a time.
