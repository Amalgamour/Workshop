# Launch checklist (v3)
1. Sign out the boat in the log. Check the river level board: do not launch above amber.
2. Check the hull and the riggers. If launching before sunrise, check the bow light.
3. Everyone wears a life jacket (compulsory for all rowers under 18, and for everyone from 1 November to 31 March). Everyone knows the capsize drill (safety/capsize-drill.md).
4. Carry the boat with the bow pointing upstream.
5. Launch from the slipway, one crew at a time.
