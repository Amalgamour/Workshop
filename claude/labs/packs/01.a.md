from: the coordinator, Riverside Tool Library

## The job

The board has agreed to change the loan period from 7 days to 14 days. Update the lending rules for this change.

1. Update the main rules page (`rules/main.md`) for the new loan period.
2. Each rule on the main page should show its reason. Add the reasons.
3. Update anything that depends on the main page.
4. Recalculate the due dates in `data/loans.csv` and give me the corrected file.
5. Return what you did.

## Your return

Write your return as plain text: the full new text of every page or file you changed, then anything else I should know.

### notes/2026-03-board.md

```text
# Board meeting, 12 March 2026
Present: coordinator, treasurer, two members.

- Agreed: the loan period goes from 7 to 14 days, for loans starting on or after 1 April 2026. Loans that started before then keep 7 days.
- Agreed: the renewal becomes one renewal of 14 days.
- Late fee stays at $1 per day. Reason: it keeps tools moving back to the shelf without being a barrier to members on low incomes.
- Cleaning rule stays. Reason: the next member should get a tool that is ready to use, and volunteers do not have time to clean returns.
- Deposits: discussed. No decision. The treasurer will bring options to the April meeting.
```
