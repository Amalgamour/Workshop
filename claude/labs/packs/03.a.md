### rules/club.md

```text
# Harbor Rowing Club: club rules
These rules apply at both boathouses. A boathouse page lists only its differences.

## 1 Opening
The boathouse opens at 6am and closes at dusk.

## 2 Rowing solo
A member may row solo if cleared: safety test passed within the last 12 months, aged 18 or over, and swim test passed.
Reason: a solo rower has no one to help them in the water.

## 3 Guests
Members may bring a guest twice a season.

## 4 Safety
Every crew must know the capsize drill before going out (see safety/capsize-drill.md).
```

### procedures/launch-v2.md

```text
# Launch checklist (v2)
1. Sign out the boat in the log.
2. Check the river level board. Do not launch above amber. (Added after the October 2024 capsize, when a crew launched on red.)
3. Check the hull and the riggers.
4. Check the bow light if launching before sunrise.
5. Everyone knows the capsize drill (safety/capsize-drill.md).
6. Carry the boat with the bow pointing upstream.
7. Launch from the slipway, one crew at a time.
```
