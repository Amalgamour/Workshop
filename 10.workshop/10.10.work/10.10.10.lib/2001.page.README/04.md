## Goal
Why: because a model asked to do mechanical work will do it differently each time.

Body: The clerk does the part a program can do: the format, the header, the skeleton, the derived lines. The model writes the why and the body. This is how a page gets written without the errors that keep coming back.

## Ground
Why: because the kernel is not finished and a model will use whatever it finds.

Body: The live kernel is not updated and is not used. The kernel draft is the reference and is read only from here. Work goes in band 2000, one slice at a time.

## A slice
Why: because a piece with no test cannot be told from a piece that reads well.

Body: A slice is one small part of the clerk. It says what compute fills, what the model fills, and the test. It is done when the test passes.

## The loop
Why: because work without a job is a guess.

Body: A job is a marker and an ask, and the director's reply under it. The model builds the smallest draft, runs the test, shows it, and stops. The director decides. Nothing here is committed, signed, approved, locked or promoted by the model.
