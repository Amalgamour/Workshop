why: A journal of the words I use on the ferry.
note: These fifty words are mine. Each one has one meaning and one entry.
ask: Start a desk, a shelf, and a book. Put these terms in the book as a journal. Then take the Sunday use.
use: Sunday copy. Slack water at dawn. The green bell was up. Lamp out waited until the quiet hour.

term: slack water
meaning: The hour the ferry can leave without fighting the tide.
entry: Left on slack water. The bow stopped pulling.

term: green bell
meaning: The buoy on the west side of the channel.
entry: The green bell was on the starboard side the whole way.

term: second whistle
meaning: The blast he gives when a skiff is crossing blind.
entry: He gave the second whistle and the skiff held still.

term: wet ledger
meaning: The notebook that rides in the left coat fold.
entry: The wet ledger took the spray and the pencil still wrote.

term: oilskin pocket
meaning: The left fold of the coat. The notebook never rides on the other side.
entry: He patted the oilskin pocket before he cast off.

term: right pocket
meaning: Coins and the extra blast. Never the notebook.
entry: The right pocket had the coins and nothing else.

term: bow lamp
meaning: The light he sets before the line leaves the post.
entry: He lit the bow lamp before the line left the post.

term: june fog
meaning: Vapor that sits on the water and leaves the rise open.
entry: June fog covered the channel and the rise stayed open.

term: hill clear
meaning: The tree on the tip is visible.
entry: The hill clear mark let him trust the bend.

term: oak point
meaning: The last mark before the bend.
entry: Oak point came up on the left and he started the bend.

term: channel turn
meaning: The bend where the current changes side.
entry: He made the channel turn with the current on the new side.

term: lee side
meaning: The side he puts the small boats when the air comes from the sunset.
entry: He put the small boats on the lee side.

term: west wind
meaning: The air that piles boats together.
entry: A west wind came up after the early trip.

term: stacked boats
meaning: Three small boats tied as one. He will not add another.
entry: Three stacked boats were enough.

term: fourth skiff
meaning: A boat he sends to the sand instead of the raft.
entry: The fourth skiff waited on the sand.

term: beach line
meaning: The rope from the post to the tree base.
entry: He checked the beach line with his boot.

term: oak root
meaning: The base that holds the shore rope.
entry: The oak root still held.

term: monday check
meaning: Post, tree base, buoy, light. In that order.
entry: Monday check found the fastener still bright.

term: piling bolt
meaning: The fastener that worked loose in the third month.
entry: He put a hand on the piling bolt.

term: march bolt
meaning: The replacement. Painted so he can see it from the deck.
entry: The march bolt showed bright against the wood.

term: red paint
meaning: The mark on anything he has repaired himself.
entry: Red paint means he fixed it.

term: deck nail
meaning: A fastener that stands proud of the boards.
entry: He hammered the deck nail before anyone stepped on.

term: boarding count
meaning: He counts feet, not papers.
entry: The boarding count was eleven feet.

term: ticket tin
meaning: The box under the bench. It is not the count.
entry: The ticket tin stayed under the bench.

term: lamp glass
meaning: The spare chimney. Wrapped in cloth.
entry: The lamp glass was still wrapped.

term: grey rag
meaning: The cloth that only touches the spare chimney.
entry: He left the grey rag in its place.

term: coffee tin
meaning: The morning box. It is not the box under the bench.
entry: The coffee tin was empty by the crate trip.

term: first crossing
meaning: The early run. No crates on this one.
entry: The first crossing carried only people.

term: freight run
meaning: The later run. Crates only if they fit under the bench.
entry: The freight run took two crates.

term: crate mark
meaning: A blue cross. Unmarked crates stay on the dock.
entry: He looked for the crate mark before lifting.

term: blue chalk
meaning: The stick in the coin fold.
entry: Blue chalk marked the second crate.

term: dock bell
meaning: The bell on the shed wall, not the buoy.
entry: The dock bell rang once for the crate trip.

term: shed key
meaning: The key on the nail behind the door.
entry: The shed key was on the nail.

term: key ring
meaning: Blast, light key, room key. Never the door nail key.
entry: His key ring had three keys.

term: house key
meaning: The key to the room over the work shed.
entry: The house key opened the room.

term: above shed
meaning: The room. The notebook is copied here on the last morning.
entry: He climbed to the above shed with the damp notebook.

term: sunday copy
meaning: He copies the damp notebook in pencil onto fresh sheets.
entry: Sunday copy took an hour.

term: dry paper
meaning: The last-morning sheets. They are the record.
entry: Dry paper held the week.

term: pencil stub
meaning: The short pencil tied to the notebook.
entry: The pencil stub was short and it wrote.

term: ledger twine
meaning: The cord on the pencil. He replaces it when it darkens.
entry: The ledger twine was still pale.

term: brown twine
meaning: Cord that is finished. It does not go back on the notebook.
entry: He dropped the brown twine in the bin.

term: tide table
meaning: The card nailed inside the door.
entry: The tide table said the early hour was near six.

term: shed card
meaning: The hours card. He does not trust a card that leaves the room.
entry: The shed card stayed on the nail.

term: passenger name
meaning: A name he writes only when someone asks.
entry: She asked him to keep her passenger name.

term: remember list
meaning: Names he was asked to keep. It is not a roll of people.
entry: He added her to the remember list.

term: no manifest
meaning: He refuses a printed roll of people.
entry: He will not carry a roll. The rule is no manifest.

term: quiet hour
meaning: The span after the last run, before the light is dark.
entry: Quiet hour was the room and the pencil.

term: lamp out
meaning: The last act. The front light goes dark.
entry: Lamp out. The front light was dark.

term: spare whistle
meaning: The blast in the coin fold.
entry: He still had the spare whistle.

term: oilskin cuff
meaning: The cuff he wipes the pencil on.
entry: He wiped the pencil on the oilskin cuff.
