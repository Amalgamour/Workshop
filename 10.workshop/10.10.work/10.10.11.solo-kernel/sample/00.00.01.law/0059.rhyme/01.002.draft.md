## Two rhymes
Kernel books `01`–`09` match that face. Book `03` is where a model meets the clerk. Books `10` and up do not rhyme.
Inside a book, page `0001`–`0009` matches that face. Page `0003` is that book's clerk question, or it is absent.
Absence is allowed. A wrong body is not. `0006` is notes. `0007` is reserved. Do not fill it. Do not call it fixture or unset.
Say book or face. Never a bare `03`.
The address order is desk, shelf, book, page, face. Widths `2.2.2.4.2`. The measure is page `0011`. This page is the lookup, not a second measure.

## The cross
The page is one axis. The face is the other.
Schema of the law is page `0001`, face `02`.
Law of schema is page `0002`, face `01`.
Same integers. Opposite questions. At the book, check both directions. `0008` face `01` and `0001` face `08` can both be valid. Do not collapse them. Do not stop after the first.

A high page rhymes too. Page `2883` face `02` is the schema of that page. Face `01` on it is the law of that page, if that face exists.
Most pages carry `03` and `04` when a model or a person needs that page. The other faces are capacity. Face `00` is not a page file. `07` stays reserved even on a high page.

## Fallback
No face `01` on the page you are in: go to page `0001`.
No face `02` on the page you are in: go to page `0002`.
Do not invent the missing face to avoid the fallback.

Those two pages point onward. About is page `0010`. New open pages start at `0020`. Pieces that are not commands live in `0051`–`0100`, this page included.
If the reserved page is missing too, the climb is `0052`. Do not invent the face.
A search for law may open page `0001`, or face `01` on a page whose title matches the keyword.

## Must-not
Treat a missing `01` as permission to write one. Leave every page `0003` empty. Collapse the cross into one number. Invent `07`.
