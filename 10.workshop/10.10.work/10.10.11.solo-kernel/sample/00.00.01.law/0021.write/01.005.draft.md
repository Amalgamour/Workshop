## Patch
A patch is minor. It dates the change on the active face and does not rewrite it. A rewrite is a new face.version. The active face stays until approve renames the draft onto it. Approve does not copy.
Do not replace an active face to save a step. That is the bleed.

## Fix
A fix is the colab return made into a write. It names the page and the sentence that changes. If the change is minor, date it on the active face. If it rewrites the face, it is FF.VVV beside the seat. A fix does not approve itself.
