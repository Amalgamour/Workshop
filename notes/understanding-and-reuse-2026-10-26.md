## Understanding to retain

Librarian exists to preserve understanding in a durable, findable working place.
The record must supply a basis from which a replacement model can reconstruct
understanding, rather than requiring a remembered conversation.

When a word or idea is incomplete, Clerk retrieves the recorded menu of candidate
meanings. Intelligence selects the contextual interpretation. If the recorded
possibilities do not resolve the problem, the matter escalates to the Director.
The resulting instruction and reasoning should be preserved for future work.
Selecting an interpretation does not itself create an authorization.

The dictionary and .brands concept support contextual meaning. Equal titles do
not establish equal identity; address and context distinguish their places.
The earlier band.grok, band.codex, and band.claude names were examples of spatial
separation, explicitly not a naming convention for future bands.

## Architectural separation

- Kernel: small, insertable universal grammar and laws for building/operating desks.
- Workshop: the application with Clerk and its structural support.
- Clerk: strict compute performing the defined mechanical work, recording state,
  and routing according to declarations made by intelligence.
- Work book: the development process, designs, batches, decisions, returns and learning.
- Repo pieces: finished reusable work, including instructions by which another
  worker can reproduce a useful result. Not merely a cache of code or a synonym
  for a new Git repository on every reuse.

## The development record and the seated instructions

The Director distinguished working batches 05.NN from a final seated 05.

- 05.NN preserves a particular development batch, replies, execution and findings.
- At completion, the slice receives a consolidated 05: the best reproducible
  instruction set, including issues a builder needs to anticipate.
- The development record is cleaned and archived with meaningful reasons and
  Director replies preserved. Cleanup is not permission to erase understanding.
- The reusable repo piece can remain active after its development book is archived.
- A Prime can ask Clerk to bring a suitable repo piece into another project as a
  local executable slice/page, with source identity, dependencies and checks.
- New use can expose new issues. Those feed proposed improvements; a newer version
  earns promotion by preserving what matters and accounting for changes.

A completion check should ask both: does the output work, and can a fresh builder
reproduce it using the seated instructions and declared dependencies? Essential
steps should not remain discoverable only by rereading development chat.
